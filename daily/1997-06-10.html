"Who is the other?"

那个他人是谁？
https://www.sahaja.live/1997-0610-public-program-cathedral-of-st-john-the-divine-manhatttan-nyc/
◇
English
But the highest thing that happens to you that you develop a new dimension in your awareness which we call as collective consciousness.
中文
但是你身上发生的最高境界，是在觉知中打开了一个全新的维度——我们称之为集体意识。
English
You become collectively conscious, Jung has talked about it, you become collective, conscious, means you start feeling others on your finger tips.
中文
你变得具有集体意识，荣格谈过这一点，你变得具有集体意识，意味着你能在指尖上感知到他人。
English
On your finger tips you can feel yourself.
中文
在你自己的指尖上，你能感受到自己。
English
Now these chakras, as you have been told, are denoted on your palms and you can feel on your finger tips your own chakras and the chakras of others, the centres of others and your own.
中文
这些轮穴，正如你所知道的，对应在手掌上，你可以在指尖上感受到自己和他人的轮穴——他人和你的各个能量中心。
English
So who is the other?
中文
那么，那个他人是谁？
English
You become collectively conscious.
中文
你变得具有集体意识了。
English
I am saying again, you become!
中文
我要再说一遍——你真的变得具有了！
English
It's not lecturing, it's not saying.
中文
这不是讲课，不是说教。
English
Then see how many problems get solved, you know.
中文
然后你看看，有多少问题就这么解决了，你知道吗？
English
For example, we indulge, as he said that we are really creating problems for our Mother Earth, but actually because we are not collectively conscious.
中文
比如，我们沉迷于各种事，就像刚才那位先生说的，我们确实在给大地母亲制造问题——但实际上是因为我们没有集体意识。
English
Once you are collectively conscious, you are in your awareness, then all the time you think that: 'What will harm others? Should I smoke or not? Why should I smoke? It will harm others. Should I start a pub or not? No, because it will harm others.'
中文
一旦你具有了集体意识，你就处在觉知之中，那时你每时每刻都会想：'什么会伤害别人？我该不该抽烟？我为什么要抽烟？它会伤害别人。我该不该开个酒吧？不该，因为它会伤害别人。'
English
All these wrong things that we are doing for money or for anything whatsoever, drop.
中文
我们为了金钱或其他任何目的所做的所有错事，自然就放下了。
English
We just don't do wrong things, just don't do.
中文
我们就是不做错事，就是不做。
English
Why?
中文
为什么？
English
Because we are collectively conscious.
中文
因为我们具有集体意识。
