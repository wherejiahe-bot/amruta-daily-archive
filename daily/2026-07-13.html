Depth achieved by setting boundaries

通过设定界限以达至深度
Source: amruta.today
◇
Your attention should have that gravity.
你的注意力应当具备这种重力。
Your attention is very important.
注意力非常重要。
A person who is easily disturbed is not a deep person.
一个容易被打扰的人不够深沉。
Or easily taken to something, like say emotions.
或者说，容易被某些事物带走，比如情绪，
Or easily to some other, say, intellectual pursuit.
或者容易投入到其他的，比如智力追求中。
As gurus there will be temptations for you.
作为导师，你会面临诱惑。
People will be challenging your state.
人们会挑战你的状态。
A person comes out with a very big intellectual ‘truth’ or something, then immediately you feel challenged about it and you will say, “All right let me handle the situation.”
有人可能提出一个看似高深的“真理”或其他知识性的主张，这时你可能会立刻感到受到了挑战，然后想：“好吧，让我来处理这个问题。”
Handle it at the Sahaja Yoga point, on your gravity.
在霎哈嘉瑜伽的视角上，在你的重心点上来处理它。
Just sit down on your gravity and see for yourself.
只需坐定于你的重心之上，自行观察。
Immediately you will know how to handle the situation.
你将立刻明白如何应对这一情况。
You need not talk to that person, you’ll handle the situation all right: not on [the] intellectual level but on your gravity.
你无需与那人争论，你可以妥善处理这一状况：不是在智力层面，而是在你的重心之上。
If the attention has the gravity, it pulls down the ego and the superego of other people.
如果注意力具有重力，它就能拉低他人的自我与超我。
Then you will not be frustrated or frantic.
这样，你就不会感到沮丧或慌乱。
Also if you can start from the outside it may work out with human beings; it works out like that.
同时，如果你能从外部开始做起，这种方法在人际交往中可能会奏效；确实有这样的效果。
Like study your behaviour how you behave towards others.
比如，观察你的行为，看看你如何对待他人。
Or study your attention how it behaves towards outside: what attracts your attention, what attracts your attention.
或是观察你的注意力如何对外界作出反应：是什么吸引了你的注意力，什么引起了你的兴趣。
Study your attention.
仔细研究你的注意力。
By that study you will be able to fix your attention in its maryada, in its boundaries.
通过这样的研究，你将能够固定你的注意力在其应有的界限之内。
Once you put the boundaries to the attention then the depth starts developing.
一旦给注意力设定了界限，深度就开始发展。
The person who has no boundaries can never have depth, he will be spreading just like that, always; no depth at all.
没有界限的人永远不会有深度，他会像那样无止境地扩散开来，完全没有深度可言。
So you have to put boundaries by understanding your attention, that, “No not more than that.”
因此，你需要通过理解自己的注意力来设定界限，告诉自己：“就到这里为止。”
“All right not more than that.”
“好吧，不能超过这个程度。”
Like some people in Sahaja Yoga I have seen, they get interested in a patient, then for them that patient becomes important.
在霎哈嘉瑜伽中，我注意到有些人会对某个病人产生兴趣，随后这个病人对他们而言变得十分重要。
Or get interested in some person that he must get Realisation: “my mother,” “my father,” “my, my” .
又或是对某个人特别关注，认为他必须获得自觉：“我的母亲”、“我的父亲”、“我的、我的…”。
And all the time they are talking about it: “my jobs,” “my clothes,” some sort of a ‘my’.
他们总是谈论这些：“我的工作”、“我的衣服”，诸如此类的“我的”。
And they don’t know how to draw the line.
但他们不知道如何划清界限。
You must know where to draw the line, is maryadas.
你必须清楚界限何在，这便是分际。
Otherwise the whole thing will fritter away.
否则，所有努力都会白费，
You will become a frivolous person.
你会变得轻浮。
There won’t be any gravity in you.
你身上将失去那份重力。
So you must know how far to go with a person when you are dealing as a guru, then you are a master.
因此，当你以导师的身份与人交往时，必须知道与人相处的分际，那时你才是真正的主宰。
But if you do not know where to draw the line, you may be crossing the line and getting into his clutches.
但如果你不知道分际何在，你可能会越界，反而落入他人的掌控之中。
So the best way to handle the situation is to get into thoughtless awareness, again and again and again and again, and you establish that clearly.
处理这种情况的最佳方式是一次又一次地进入无思虑的觉醒状态，并明确地建立起这一点。
