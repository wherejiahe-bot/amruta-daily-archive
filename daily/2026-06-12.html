The reason for rationality

理性、情感与智慧
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
Rationalism, Emotionalism And WisdomPublic Program，Caxton Hall, London (England)Talk Language: English | Transcript (English) – VERIFIED
理性、情感与智慧公开讲座，地点：（英国）伦敦卡克斯顿大厅谈话语言：英语 | 文本记录（英语）- 已验证
Rationality, emotionality and wisdom, Caxton Hall, London (UK), 12 June 1978
理性、情感与智慧，1978年6月12日，（英国）伦敦卡克斯顿大厅
…Then what can rationality do? There must be some reason for having rationality in us [and] why God has given us rationality. He doesn’t waste his diamonds like that!
那么理性又能做什么呢？我们的理性必定有其存在的理由，以及为什么神赋予我们理性，祂不会如此浪费祂的钻石！
Rationality gives us the sense to understand that we cannot reach there through rationality.
理性让我们意识到，我们无法仅凭理性达到那个境界。
Because unless and until this thing happens to you, you are not going to believe in it. You are not going to believe in it that, this rationality, on which we depend, which is our support, which is we; we all the time think that our identification is complete with rationality. And we are very rational and we are proud of our rationality also. So then, you reach a point, through rationality, where you understand that this is not the horse which will be useful any further. This is the use of rationality!
因为除非这件事发生在你身上，否则你不会相信它。你不会相信这种理性，一直以来我们所依赖、作为支撑、视为是我们本身的这种理性。我们总是认为自己的身份认同完全建立在理性之上。我们非常理性，并且也为自己拥有理性而自豪。然而，通过理性，你会达到一个理解的点，即这匹马再也无法帮助你更进一步了——这就是理性的用途所在！
One way it takes you to that culminating point where you think it is to be discarded or cannot be depended upon.
一方面，理性会带你到达一个顶点，让你认为它应当被抛弃或无法再依赖。
On the other side rationality gives you a view which can be related later on, when you get the experience. You’ll start understanding why rationality was failing you. So, it’s a teacher on a negative way. It’s a teacher on a negative way.
另一方面，理性提供了一种视角，这种视角可以在你获得体验后关联起来。你会开始理解为什么理性曾经让你失望。因此，从某种意义上说，它是一个以否定方式教导你的老师。是的，它以一种否定的方式成为教师。
But it is essential because man was given freedom and he started using his freedom, he started using his rationality.
但这是必不可少的，因为人被赋予了自由，并开始使用他的自由，开始运用他的理性。
When he reaches that point, then he starts depending on his wisdom. And wisdom is the balance between his emotionality and rationality, in the centre of it, between his heart and his mind. It is a centre somewhere there, in the central, or we say in the fulcrum.
当他达到某一点时，他开始依靠智慧。智慧是他情感与理性的平衡点，位于他的心与脑之间，处于中央某个位置，或者我们可以说是在支点（fulcrum）上。
So, if you are too much on the emotionality, or too much on the rationality, you have to move further into the centre and balance it at a point, then only you are in wisdom, and that’s how you rise within yourself.
所以，如果你过于情绪化或过于理性，你需要进一步向中心位置移动，在某一点上平衡它们，只有这样你才处于智慧之中，也只有这样才能实现自身的升进。
Any extreme behaviour, on the left or the right, or dependence, is not going to help you.
任何极端的行为，无论是偏向左还是右，或是过度依赖，都不会对你有所帮助。
This you can understand through even emotionality. A person who is very emotional (sometimes ?), he sort of ‘loves’ someone, loves, you see; and he goes ahead with sincerity after that love, and he gets into infatuation. And then he realises, “Oh! This is not joy. How could I fail?” So he says, “I have a broken heart.” It’s better that he realises that.
这一点甚至可以通过情感来理解。一个非常情绪化的人（有时可能是如此），他有点“爱”上某人，他真诚地追求这份爱，但最终陷入了迷恋。然后他意识到，“哦！这不是快乐。我怎么会失败呢？”于是他说，“我的心碎了。”他能意识到这一点其实是好的。
So he turns to his head. But he turns to his head to such an extreme that he gives up his broken heart completely.
于是他转向理智。但他转向理智到了极端的地步，以至于他完全放弃了自己那颗破碎的心。
It is in the centre, in the centre of your heart and your brain, lies this fulcrum point. Rationality and emotionality are two extremes, but in the centre of it.
智慧在中心位置上，在你的心脏和大脑之间的中心位置上。理性和情感是两个极端，但智慧处于这两者之间的心脏与大脑的中心位置。
So, wisdom is an abstract word. What is wisdom? You ask somebody, “Is it wisdom?” “This is wisdom? That is wisdom?”
因此，智慧是一个抽象的概念。什么是智慧？你问某人，“这是智慧吗？”“这是智慧吗？那是智慧吗？”
Then how do you understand the wisdom? By discarding, by saying, “No, this is not wisdom,” “This is not wisdom,” “This cannot be wisdom.” Here you use both your emotionality and your rationality, when you start deciding that this is true.
那么，你如何理解智慧呢？通过摒弃，通过说“不，这不是智慧”，“这不是智慧”，“这不可能是智慧”。当你开始判断这是真理的时候，你同时运用了你的情感和理性。
That’s why I said that these are negative teachers: in the sense that they drive you towards positivity. In the sense that they drive you towards the Reality. They teach you that this is not the correct thing.
这就是为什么我说这些都是负面的老师；它们的意义在于促使你走向正面，引领你接近实相（Reality）。它们教你认识到，某些事物并非正确的。
Take any one of your ‘political enterprises’, [as] I would call them, ‘political institutions’ as you call them: take communism, take capitalism, take all these sorts of isms that you have all around.
以我所称的“政治事业”或你们所谓的“政治体制”为例，比如共产主义、资本主义，以及你们周围所有这类“主义”。
All experiments, all your political experiments, which you have found through your rational approaches – they cannot give you a solution.
所有这些尝试，所有你们通过理性途径发现的政治实验——它们都无法为你提供一个最终的解决方案。
Democracy fails, communism also fails. Any other monarchy fails. In the same way demonocracy also fails.
民主制度会有其局限，共产主义也会遇到困境。任何君主制或其他形式的政体也都会遭遇失败。同样民主制度也会失败。
Everything fails! Then you realise that, “Is there any truth in going through these things?” “Is this the way we are going to reach anywhere?” Then you start understanding that, “No, no. We all have made some mistakes somewhere.” But this is not possible for people who are running the race. [For those] who are running the race it is not possible. For example, I cannot talk to bureaucrats – impossible – because they are holding, they are carrying the bags. So, some are democratic, some are communistic, I don’t know what sort of whims they have.
一切尝试似乎都会归于失败！然后你开始意识到：“经历这一切真的有真理可循吗？”“这是我们能达到目标的途径吗？”于是你开始明白：“不，不对。我们在某个地方都犯了一些错误。”但对于那些在竞赛中奔跑的人来说，这是难以接受的。对于正在竞赛中的人来说，这是不可能的。例如，我无法与官僚们交流——不可能的——因为他们固守着自己的立场，背负着各自的包袱。所以，有的坚持民主，有的信奉共产主义，我不清楚他们有着怎样的突发奇想。
So these big Johnnies are not going to listen to us. No, they cannot! Because for them that is identified. To them that, what they call, is a ‘perfect’ political institution, is the goal of life, and everybody must come to it!
所以，这些大人物是不会听我们的。不，他们做不到！因为对他们来说，那些已被认同的东西。对他们而言，他们所谓的“完美”政治体制是人生的目标，所有人都应趋向于此！
That’s why, when these different ideologies and different “isms” start flowering, you find that they have not yet reached any flowering and they just shrivel up, they lose their existence, because rationality doesn’t give you that complete picture.
这就是为什么，当这些不同的意识形态和各种“主义”开始兴起时，你会发现它们其实并未真正开花结果，它们只是枯萎了，失去了存在感，因为理性不能给你一个完整的图景。
It doesn’t give you the complete idea as to how far man can progress with rationality and how far he is going to achieve the result. What is the result? What do we expect? Is to have joy.
理性不能提供一个完整的想法，告诉我们仅凭理性人类能够进步到何种程度，以及我们究竟能否通过它达到预期的结果。结果是什么？我们期待的是什么？答案是拥有喜乐。
But any democracies or any communistic countries or any despotic countries, anywhere you go, you will find the people have no joy; they have forgotten how to even smile! You have to give them an injection to make them laugh!
但在任何民主国家、共产主义国家或专制国家，无论你走到哪里，你会发现人们缺乏喜乐；他们甚至忘记了如何微笑！你得给他们打一剂“笑针”才能让他们笑！
So, this rationality gives us an understanding that all our endeavours or all our enterprises which have been brought forth through rationality are not fulfilling. They show something but finish off, wither away.
因此，理性让我们明白，所有基于理性产生的努力和事业并没有达到真正的满足。它们可能展示出某些东西，但最终会消逝、凋零。
Same with our emotionality like we have, we have ‘faith’ in God. You have faith in God, so anybody who says, “I am God” people run after such a person. There are many who are crazy like that!
情感也是如此，比如我们对神的“信仰”。你相信神，所以当有人宣称“我是神”时，人们就会追逐这样的人。像那样疯狂的人有很多！
The other day only, I was watching a TV show where they showed a man who said, “I am Christ” and he was a follower of Hitler. And he was killing people and he was doing all kinds of things and he had a big following!
就在前几天，我观看一个电视节目，节目里有个自称“我是基督”的人，他是希特勒的追随者。他杀人，做出了种种恶行，他有一大批追随者！
And the people were crying and weeping for him when he was facing his trial. One cannot explain these things, how these things happen.
当他面临审判时，那些人还在为他哭泣哀求。这样的现象实在难以解释，这些事情究竟是如何发生的。
How can God be that poorly?
神怎能如此不堪呢？
And how could people who are talking about God could be like that? Because they play on the emotionality of the people. They think, “Oh I feel very fine”. “I have seen many good people who come here,” “Oh, I feel very fine in his company, that guru met me,” “Oh, I feel very happy here!”
谈论神的人又怎能如此表现？因为他们利用了人们的感情。这些人想，“哦，我觉得非常好”，“我在这里见到了很多好人”，“哦，在那位导师的陪伴下我感到非常愉快”，“哦，我在这里感到非常快乐！”
What did he do? How do you feel fine?
他做了什么？你感觉怎么样？
What does it appeal to you? It is such a subtle type of diversion from the Reality. It’s such a subtle type, that you cannot even make out.
它什么地方吸引你？这是一种非常精微的逃避现实的方式。这是一种非常精微的类型，以至于你甚至无法分辨。
Specially rational people cannot, they are befooled the first!
尤其是理性的人，他们最容易上当受骗！
The so called ego-oriented people are befooled the first with emotionality. To them anybody who comes with emotions, you know – stands up with very big emotions and tears in the eyes and shouts at the loud speaker and, as if he is just going to die with the pangs, or something like that (laughing) – appeals so much [to them]. Because that’s the balance for their rational minds, that they fall a trap.
所谓的以自我为中心的人首先会被情绪所欺骗。对于他们来说，任何带着强烈情绪出现的人——你知道的——站起来时带着巨大的情绪、眼里含着泪水，在扩音器前大喊，好像他正因痛苦而即将死去，或者类似的表现（笑）——会非常吸引他们。因为这是对他们理性思维的一种平衡，他们很容易落入这样的陷阱。
So this kind of a emotionality also can go to another extreme of madness! Like you have seen people singing songs of separation with God. It sickens me sometimes the way, you know? It’s sickening! I don’t know how people like it.
因此，这种情绪化也可能走向疯狂的另一个极端！比如你看到有人唱着与神分裂的歌曲。有时候这让我感到恶心，你知道吗？真是令人厌恶！我不明白人们怎么会喜欢这样。
The other day we had that musician who came to me, so I said, “You sing me a song”. He said “What type of song, Mother, You want to hear?” I said, “Sing us some song of joy”. “Song of joy?”
前几天有个音乐家来找我，于是我说：“你给我唱首歌吧。”他说：“母亲，您想听哪种类型的歌曲？” 我说：“给我们唱一首欢乐的歌吧。”“欢乐的歌？”
He was lost! He said, “They have produced no song of joy for the last twenty years in this country, anywhere!” None of them in the English society or the Western people.
他愣住了！他说：“这个国家已经有二十年没有制作过任何欢乐的歌曲了，任何地方都没有！”无论是英国社会还是西方人，都是如此。
I said, “No song of joy?” He picked up something somewhere, and which also had a string of crying and weeping in it. I said, “Now there is no absolute joy anywhere!”
我说：“没有欢乐的歌？” 他在某个地方找了一首，但那里面也夹杂着哭泣和哀伤的情绪。我说：“现在到处都没有纯粹的欢乐了！”
Just think of it! The emotionality has reached to such an extent that you have lost all praise of joy, completely. So, you cannot depend on that kind of an emotionality that – in the church, you go and somebody stands on the pulpit and says, “Oh, I am like that!”, you see, with all the vibrating sounds and all that drama of emotionality. And you say “Oh! What a thing! Oh, I was very happy!”
想想看！这种情绪化已经到了如此严重的地步，你们完全失去了对喜悦的赞美。所以，你不能依赖那种在教堂里——你去到那里，有人站在讲坛上说，“哦，我是那样的！”你知道的，带着所有震动的声音和那戏剧性的情绪表现。然后你说：“哦！多棒啊！哦，我很高兴！”
If such a thing is accepted in life, I can tell you, be rest assured, that when such a thing appeals to you, you must know that you are not facing the Reality at all. You are not prepared to face the Reality.
如果生活中接受了这样的事情，我可以告诉你，可以肯定的是，当这样的事情吸引你时，你必须知道你根本没有面对实相。你没有准备好面对实相。
You do not want to face it! You might give any excuse about it. You’d like to identify yourself as,“Oh, I am too much of a rational person. I am too rational, I cannot accept this. This is beyond me, I just can’t!”.Or you may even say,“I am English, bit too English I cannot accept it!”
你不想面对它！你可能会为此找任何借口。你可能希望将自己定义为，“哦，我是一个太过理性的人。我太理性了，我不能接受这个。这超出了我的理解范围，我就是不行！”或者你甚至可能说，“我是英国人，太英国化了，我不能接受这个！”
Or somebody may say that, “I am too much of an Indian, I cannot accept this kind of a thing.” But when you say you are an Indian, first of all, you are misidentifying, and secondly you are misidentifying with one extreme or the other extreme.
又或者有人会说：“我太印度化了，我不能接受这种事情。”但当你自称是印度人时，首先，你是在错误地认同自己，其次，你是在错误地认同某一个极端或另一个极端。
So before starting about your seeking, you have to understand yourself – which extreme you are standing. Very important it is. Very important it is to understand this part, “Where do I stand before I ask for Self-realisation?”
所以在开始你的寻求之前，你必须了解自己——你站在哪个极端。这是非常重要的。理解这部分非常重要：“在我寻求自觉之前，我站在哪里？”
The another thing about Self-realisation [which] is very important, specially in the Western mind, which I must tell you – I say ‘Western’ because Eastern have not yet gone to that end of rationality, that’s why – that, one has to understand that we are asking for Truth; and Truth is not going to fall at your feet.
关于自觉，还有一点非常重要，特别是在西方人的思维中，我必须告诉你——我之所以提到“西方”，是因为东方人还没有走到理性的那个极端，所以——有一点必须明白，那就是我们在追求真理；而真理不会俯首在你的脚下。
It is not a clinic opened out here where you have paid the money and you go to the doctor and tell him, “You – I have fever despite the fact [that] you gave me medicine.” And you report to him that, “What is this? I paid you ten pounds and nothing has happened to me so far, what about my health?” You cannot demand from the Truth anything like that. Now this is difficult for a Western mind to understand! To me also they treat in the same manner! While I’m not good at receiving something.
这里不是一家诊所，你付了钱就可以去见医生，并告诉他：“你——尽管你给了我药，我还是发烧。”然后你向他报告说：“这是怎么回事？我给了你十镑，到现在为止我身上什么变化也没有，我的健康状况如何呢？”你不能这样向真理要求什么。现在这对于西方人的思维来说很难理解！他们也用同样的方式对待我！虽然我不擅长于接受某些东西。
So I must warn you, nobody is going to fall at your feet or run after you that you get your Self-realisation. No. That absolutely must be understood by all of you.
所以我必须警告你，没有人会跪在你脚下或追着你要给你自觉。不，这一点你们所有人都必须绝对理解。
You cannot purchase it. You cannot pay for it. You cannot control it. You cannot ask for it. It is the grace which has to work it out, which human beings cannot understand, you see.
你无法购买它，无法为此付费，无法控制它，也无法要求它。这是只有恩典才能成就的，人类难以理解，你看。
We have never done like that, we have never seen like that. Everything is a shopping with us.
我们从未这样做过，也从未见过这样的事。对我们来说，一切都象是在购物。
So our shopping style of mind has to be changed a little. We are not shopping God. There is no shopping involved here.
因此，我们的购物式思维需要稍微改变一下。我们不是在购买神。这里没有购物的成分。
Whenever I meet people I find this attitude in them. As if you have gone to the river Ganges to fill the bucket and the bucket, instead of putting from the side where there is the place for the water to enter in, you are putting from the other side. And then you say, “How is it Mother，I didn’t get it? There must be some fake about you.” “Yes, there is. So please leave me alone!”
每当我遇到人们时，我发现他们都有这样的态度。就好像你要去恒河用桶装水，而水没有从有空间的那一侧放入，而是从另一侧放进去。然后你说：“母亲，这是怎么回事？我没有得到它，你一定是假的。”“是的，没错。所以请让我一个人待着吧！”
It is difficult because we are used to pampering. Even doctors pamper you; they tell us lies. Even if there is nothing wrong, they’ll give medicinal water injection to say you are sick. Because they have to do their business! Everybody pampers you, tells lies. But here there’s no question [of that]. There’s no need to be pampered.
这很难，因为我们习惯了被纵容。甚至医生也会纵容你；他们会对我们撒谎。即使没有任何问题，他们也会给你打生理盐水注射，告诉你，你生病了。因为他们得做生意！每个人都宠着你，说着谎言。但在这里，没有这样的问题，没有纵容。
You must know that those who pamper you, will never heal you. They are doing it because they want to make money out of you. They want to befool you. They want to take advantage of you. They will never tell you the truth. Why should they?
你必须知道，那些纵容你的人永远不会治愈你。他们这样做是因为他们想从你身上赚钱。他们想要愚弄你。他们想利用你。他们永远不会告诉你真相。为什么他们要这么做呢？
If they tell you the Truth you will never pay them money because Truth cannot be purchased through money. They will never say this. They’ll pay attention to your purse, to your position.
如果他们告诉你真相，你就永远不会给他们钱，因为真相是无法用金钱买到的。他们永远不会这么说。他们会关注你的钱包、你的地位。
They are not bothered as to say that the human race has reached a stage where it has to jump, it has to break through. It has to get into that collective consciousness. It has to become that. It’s an important thing. That is the most important thing.
他们并不关心人类已经到达了一个必须跳跃、必须突破的阶段。人类必须进入那种集体意识。必须成为那样。这是件重要的事。那是最重要的事。
They are very low people, but they appeal to you because they appeal to your baser self. Even rationality is a baser self in man, take it from me. Through rationality you can do all kinds of baser things.
他们是卑微的人，但他们吸引你是因为他们触动了你较低层次的自我。即使是理性，在人身上也是一种较低层次的自我，相信我。通过理性，你可以做出各种低劣的事情。
Too much rationality can give you ideas [of] how to plan, how to rob a bank. What perfect plan is this? How to rob a bank, how to kill a President, how to disturb somebody who is doing something good.
过度的理性可以给你各种计划的主意，比如如何抢劫银行。这是多么完美的计划？如何抢劫银行，如何刺杀总统，如何扰乱正在做善事的人。
It’s a fact that they do this through rationality.
事实上，他们正是通过理性来做这些事情的。
And people wade through all that without feeling any compunction of their conscience or anything – “So what?”
而人们在经历这一切时，不会对自己的良心感到任何愧疚或其他——“那又怎样？”
So even rationality, if somebody appeals to you for God, you should know you are misled. Because [it’s] very nice to pamper your ego by saying, “Oh, what an intelligent man [you are]! You know, you are very intelligent!” – damned forever! “Oh, you are very well read!” – damned forever. Never believe it.
所以，即使有人以理性的名义向你兜售通往神的道路，你也应该知道你被误导了。因为通过说“哦，你是一个多么聪明的人啊！你知道，你非常聪明！”来满足你的自我，是非常惬意的——但这会让你永远受诅咒！“哦，你学识渊博！”——同样会让你永远受诅咒。永远不要相信这些。
You cannot read the Truth in the books. You cannot understand God through your brains.
你无法在书本上读到真理。你无法通过你的大脑理解神。
If you have such ideas, you cannot get to Him. How much was the reading of our Lord Jesus Christ? How many professorships He had? What school He went to?
如果你有这样的想法，你就无法接近祂。我们的主耶稣基督读了多少书？祂拥有多少教授职位？祂上过哪所学校？
And all the saints of the world with him, any one of them, how much did they study? So keep away your rationality, outside there, just like your shoes! Be humble.
世界上所有的圣人，包括祂在内，无论哪一个，他们学习了多少？所以，请将你的理性放在一边，就像你把鞋子放在门外一样！要谦卑。
This of course doesn’t appeal because rationality pampers the ego and the ego is a big balloon, you see. You press it from one side, from the other side it comes to attack. But I am quite clever and I am a Mother. I am a Mother, so I know how get it down slowly.
当然，这一点可能不会吸引人，因为理性滋养了自我，而自我就像一个大气球，你看到了。你从一侧挤压它，它就会从另一侧反击过来。但我相当机智，同时我也是一个母亲。作为母亲，我知道如何慢慢地把它降下来。
You are not your ego by any chance. You are neither your superego. You are joy. You are eternal. You have lost it, so please find it. And don’t dwell upon these out-of-date ideas.
你绝非偶然地成为你的自我，也不是你的超我。你是喜乐，你是永恒的。你失去了它，所以请找到它。不要沉溺于这些过时的想法中。
I pamper you, but on Reality, on Reality and not on something that is falsehood.
我确实会宠爱你，但这建立在实相之上，而不是基于虚假的事物。
You must have seen in the history that whenever a great sage came, a great incarnation came on this Earth, they could not talk to intellectual people.
你一定在历史中看到过，每当伟大的圣贤，伟大的化身降临人间时，他们往往无法与那些过分理智的人交流。
Kabirdas, again I would say, who was a very outspoken fellow, very outspoken, he says, “Padhi, padhi pandita moorakha bhai,” meaning that, “By reading the learned became damn fools!”
再比如卡比尔，他是一个非常坦率的人，他说，“Padhi, padhi pandita moorakha bhai”，这句话的意思是，“读书读得越多，学者们反而变得越愚蠢！”
And the Hindi poets, you see, even today, criticise him that his language was very vulgar, was very rude; [that] he was very a crude person. They call him ‘Sadhukkadi’. Whatever you may say, but he said the Truth.
而那些印地语诗人，你看，即便是今天，也（有人）批评他的语言非常粗俗，非常粗鲁；说他是个很粗鄙的人。他们称他为‘Sadhukkadi’。无论你怎么说，但他说了真话。
And this is the big Himalayan task for you, I can tell you this much.
我可以告诉你，这对你来说是一项巨大的喜马拉雅山般的任务
With all the rational people you are trying to solve the problem. Everybody very ‘capable’. They will talk like this, “I believe.”
你试图用所有理性的人来解决问题。每个人都非常“能干”。他们会这样讲，“我相信”。
Now, what do you believe? What do you know? Big things, you know! “I believe that this should be done!” Big things! Lectures! What do you know? Do you know that not even a leaf can move by your beliefs and faiths and by your talking and jabbering and thinking? Nothing!
现在，你相信什么？你知道什么？大事件，你知道的！“我相信应该这样做！”大事件！演讲！你知道什么？你知道吗，即使一片树叶也不会因为你的信念、信仰、言语、喋喋不休和思考而移动？什么都不会！

是祂，是祂在推动这个世界上的一切。是祂在管理、组织一切。是祂照料着每一件事物，是祂创造了你，也是祂创造了这个伟大的宇宙。这不是你或你那愚蠢的理性所能做到的。

请尝试，努力去理解。
Because I want you to find that which is joy, which is beautiful, which is great.
因为我希望你能找到那份喜乐，那份美好，那份伟大。

带着开放的心态来。但你不能只是命令说：“我们知道这不是神的国度！”或者说，“那不是神的国度！”或者说，“这不是真理！”你要自己去看。要谦卑下来！到目前为止，你找到祂了吗？
