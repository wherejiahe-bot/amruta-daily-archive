Shouldering Sahaja Yoga

肩负起霎哈嘉瑜伽
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
Today we have decided to have a puja of Virata in the land of Shri Krishna.
今天我们决定在锡吕·克里希纳的土地上举行宇宙大我的普祭。
As you know, in the growth of Shri Vishnu’s manifestation, He comes into ten incarnations and ultimately He manifests Himself as Virat. Virat is the brain of the being which can call as God Almighty, so the whole central nervous system is worked through by Shri Krishna as Vishnu, then all these incarnations, then Shri Krishna and ultimately as Virat.
正如你知道的，在锡吕•毗湿奴的显化过程中，祂曾降世十次，最终祂以宇宙大我本身的形象显现。宇宙大我是这个可以称为全能之神的存有的大脑，因此整个中枢神经系统是通过锡吕•克里希纳作为锡吕•毗湿奴的形象运作的，然后是这些化身，接着是锡吕•克里希纳，最终是宇宙大我。
This is the development of our brain and when we are worshipping Virata, we have to know that within us also we have got the manifestation of this power of Virat. That manifestation which we have we can call it as Virata if Shri Krishna is Mahavirata.
这是我们大脑的发展过程，当我们崇拜宇宙大我时，我们必须知道，在我们内部也拥有这种宇宙大我力量的显现。如果锡吕•克里希纳是伟大的宇宙大我的话，我们所拥有的这种宇宙大我的力量的显现也可以称为宇宙大我。
You all know about Virata much more than I can tell you today because is the whole, is the totality. And the totality of everything, if that is Virata, then that is in your brain, but reality is in your heart. So, the totality you may see, you may witness, the reality is the subtlety behind it. So, the brain which is not ruled by the heart, which is not nourished by the heart, is a very dangerous thing, because it creates extroversion, and such a person who is without any heart tries to do things, becomes very ruthless and can be very dangerous.
你们都知道的关于宇宙大我的事情比我今天能告诉你们的多得多，因为它是整体，是全部。如果万物的整体是宇宙大我，那么它就在你们的大脑中，但实相却在你们的心中。所以，你们可能会看到或见证到总体，而实相则是其背后的精微之处。因此，不受心灵支配、不受心灵滋养的大脑是非常危险的，因为它会产生外向性，而一个没有心灵的人努力做事时，会变得非常无情，并且可能非常危险。
It could be the other way also within us; that if we allow our heart to rule us, only live with our emotions and not to use our rationality, then we can become really very dangerous people to ourselves. In the sense, we become lethargic, we become indulgent into wrong sort of things. And today what one feels that in America there’s more dominance of the left side than of the right.
也可能与我们内在的情况相反；如果我们允许自己的心灵主宰我们，只生活在情感中而不运用理性，那么我们对自己来说可能会变得真的非常危险。从某种意义上说，我们会变得懒惰，沉溺于错误的事物中。如今在美国，人们感觉到偏左脉比偏右脉更有优势。
Right side is our attention and the right side of attention is, we can say, is the liver of God Almighty. So now when we have to talk of the totality, when we are part and parcel of the totality, and now we are awakened to that existence of ours as the part and parcel of the whole, then we have to realize to become the whole we have to do something about it. Specially in the land of Shri Krishna we have to work out this tremendous task of creating Viratas out of Sahaja Yogis.
右脉是我们的注意力，而注意力的右脉，我们可以称之为全能之神的肝脏。因此，当我们谈论整体时，当我们是整体的一部分，我们现在觉醒到自己作为整体一部分的存在，那么我们必须意识到要成为整体，我们必须为此做些什么。特别是在锡吕•克里希纳的土地上，我们必须完成这个创造 Viratas 的艰巨任务，使 Sahaja Yogis 成为 Viratas。
After going through the various experiences of American Sahaja Yoga, I’ve reached a conclusion that we have to talk more frankly about things than we have been talking before. Why there’s such a failure of Sahaja Yoga in this great country while there are so many seekers.
在经历了美国霎哈嘉瑜伽的各种体验之后，我得出了一个结论，即我们必须比以前更坦率地讨论问题。为什么在这样一个伟大的国家，尽管有如此多的寻求者，霎哈嘉瑜伽却失败了。
The main point is that we do not realize that the left side is overpowering this country very much. There are people who are active, but the percentage is very low because of too much of affluence, too much of freedom, the idea of “what’s wrong in anything.”
主要问题是，我们没有意识到左侧在这个国家占据着压倒性的优势。虽然有一些活跃的人，但由于过度富裕、过度自由以及‘任何事有什么不对’的想法，这个比例非常低。”
People are going towards shamelessness, immoral life, self-indulgent. And this self-indulgence, to protect that, people always develop a kind of a ego which has no dynamism in it.
人们正走向无耻、不道德的生活，自我放纵。为了保护这种自我放纵，人们总是培养出一种没有活力的自我。
So, now the problem lies with Sahaja Yogis, not with Sahaja Yoga as such. When you see that the land is not so fertile and here you have to work very hard, the responsibility of all the Sahaja Yogis in America is to go deep. You can see wherever the land is not fertile the trees go very deep down, they dig very deep into the Mother Earth for the sap to be sucked in. And the same way, they grow very high, because the strength of their deepening gives them this great height. So they grow very tall and also they are very deep.
所以，现在的问题在于霎哈嘉瑜伽士，而不是霎哈嘉瑜伽本身。当你看到这片土地不够肥沃时，你必须非常努力地工作。所有美国霎哈嘉瑜伽士的责任是深入内心。你可以看到，无论在哪里，如果土地不够肥沃，树木就会深深扎根，它们会深入大地吸取养分。同样地，由于它们的根扎得很深，它们能长得非常高。因此，它们既高大又深邃。
In the same way, the Sahaja Yogis of America have to work out because here it’s so much superficiality and so much of frivolousness and also no meaning to their lives. They have no meanings at all and they are just frittering away their lives with all nonsensical ideas about their emotions and their love and all those things.
同样地，美国的霎哈嘉瑜伽士必须有所作为，因为这里充满了表面性和轻浮，他们的生活毫无意义。他们没有任何目标，只是把生命浪费在关于情感、爱情等方面的各种无意义的想法上。
So, what we need today are Sahaja Yogis who are right-sided, those who will produce results, those who will be dynamic. They don’t have to worry that somebody is right-sided and he’ll become right-sided. Actually, that is what is needed today. Unless and until you have people who are dynamic we cannot work it out. It’s a reaction in this country as well as in England, in many countries who dominated other people.
所以，今天我们所需要的，是那些能够产生结果、充满活力的右倾霎哈嘉瑜伽士。他们不必担心某人变得右倾后会怎样。实际上，这正是今天所需要的。除非拥有充满活力的人，否则我们无法实现目标。这是在美国以及英国等多个曾经统治其他国家的国家的一种反应。
Like, you see, in this place which was – actually, originally belonged to some tribes of Indians, and the English, the Spanish and all these Europeans came, killed them and occupied here. It’s a highway robbery, absolutely a highway robbery. Because of their arrogance and their aggressiveness, they could not see that they were doing such a horrible thing. I mean, why call any names of Hitler, they’re even worse than Hitler, because at least Hitler was taken to task, he was condemned and people know that it was a mistake.
比如，这个地方——实际上是属于一些印第安部落的，而英国人、西班牙人和其他欧洲人来了，杀死了他们并占领了这里。这是一种公然的抢劫，绝对的抢劫。由于他们的傲慢和侵略性，他们看不到自己所做的是多么可怕的事情。我的意思是，何必去指责希特勒，他们甚至比希特勒还要恶劣，因为至少希特勒受到了谴责，人们知道那是一个错误。
But what about those who came all the way from there and killed so many people here and nicely having a very nice time everywhere as great Americans and now they are trying to show off everywhere. Actually it’s such a sinful thing that is done that all these souls which have died I think have possessed them, and now today America is on the left hand side. The right hand side of Americans is finished now, they are on the left hand side, guilty, feeling guilty, they are taking to indulgences.
但是，那些从远方来到这里，杀害了如此多人，并且到处享受美好生活的人呢？他们实际上是在做一件罪恶的事，这些死去的灵魂我认为已经附身在他们身上，而现在美国已经走上了左道。右脉的美国人现在消失了，他们现在处于左脉，感到内疚，沉溺于各种享乐之中。
Once you go to the right side, you see, you are very disciplined and you become very ascetic by temperament, I should say, and you want to dominate others and you want to capture. But today the situation of England and America is just the same. In England the same thing I find with Sahaja Yogis – they are just lovey-dovey types, you see, and all they love Me and love Me and love Me. [Laughter] And I just don’t understand. What’s this going on? They all love Me and they’ll bring flowers for Me, finished. Is this the way we are going to work out Sahaja Yoga in that England and in this America? “Mother, I love you very much.” So what?
一旦你转向右脉，你会发现你变得非常有纪律，可以说性格上也变得更加苦行，你会想要支配他人，想要征服。但今天的英美两国的情况也是如此。在英国，我发现霎哈嘉瑜伽士也是如此——他们都是那种过于温柔、过于多情的人，他们只是一味地爱我、爱我、爱我。[笑声] 我真的不明白这是怎么回事。他们全都爱我，他们会为我带来鲜花，然后就完了。这就是我们在英国和美国推广霎哈嘉瑜伽的方式吗？‘母亲，我非常爱你。’那又怎样？
So, to become one part and parcel of the whole, a drop has to become the ocean, you have to become great people, you have to be responsible. And this lovey-dovey business that you have has to go out of your mind completely, otherwise Sahaja Yoga cannot work out in these two countries and you’ll be cursed one after another.
因此，要成为整体的一部分，一滴水必须成为海洋，你们必须成为伟大的人，必须承担责任。你们心中这种过于温柔、过于多情的态度必须完全消失，否则霎哈嘉瑜伽在这两个国家就无法推行，你们将一个接一个地受到诅咒。
There are shocks after shocks that are coming to this country as well as to England. But people are not learning any lessons from them, still it is all right; we get this disease, all right; then we glorify it. If we are suffering from ecological difference, still we are glorified. If you are using too much of machinery out of balance, still we are glorified. Because they just want self-indulgence. Just for self-indulgence they don’t want to see the point that what is going wrong with this country is that, that they do not see anything that is reality. And the reality, though it is in the heart, though it works in the heart, it has to act. This has to act and if it does not act, this kind of a passive behavior is not going to help you.
这个国家以及英国正在经历一波接一波的冲击。但人们并没有从中吸取任何教训，依然认为一切都好；我们得了这种病，依然认为一切都好；然后我们美化它。如果我们遭受生态差异的影响，我们仍然将其美化。如果我们过度使用机械导致失衡，我们仍然将其美化。因为他们只是想要自我放纵。仅仅为了自我放纵，他们不愿意看到这个国家的问题所在，即他们看不到任何现实。而现实虽然存在于内心，虽然在内心起作用，但它必须付诸行动。如果不付诸行动，这种被动的行为是不会帮助到你们的。
Now at this old age of Mine I’m traveling from places to places and I’m doing so much. Now let us think about it: what have we done for Sahaja Yoga? Those who see you, see you as sometimes as just some hippies with dishevelled hairs and something that kind of a thing. This is the report I have, from people who came to your centers and things like that. “Oh, they are a set of hippies, you see,” just like that.
现在我在这个年纪，还在各地旅行，并做了很多事情。现在让我们思考一下：我们为霎哈嘉瑜伽做了什么？那些见到你们的人，有时会把你们看作是一群头发凌乱的嬉皮士。这是我从去过你们中心的人那里得到的反馈。‘哦，他们就是一群嬉皮士，’ 就是这样的。
So the first thing a Sahaja Yogi has to do, has to make up something of his personality, in America is very important. They are studying, they are out of job, they are doing this, that. That sort of people are not going to help Sahaja Yoga at all, they’ll be liabilities on Sahaja Yoga. If you really want to do something you have to become something. You have to study, you have to become something, you have to have some sort of a degree, diploma, something attached to you, you have to mean something. Hippies cannot run Sahaja Yoga in America; they may run better in India but not here.
所以，一个霎哈嘉瑜伽士首先要做的，就是塑造自己的个性，在美国这一点非常重要。他们有的在学习，有的失业，有的在做这做那。这样的人对霎哈嘉瑜伽没有任何帮助，反而会成为负担。如果你真的想有所作为，你就必须成为某个人物。你必须学习，必须取得一些学位、文凭，或者有些东西能够证明你的价值，你必须有所成就。嬉皮士在美国不能经营霎哈嘉瑜伽；或许在印度可以，但在这里不行。
So, one has to realize that what has gone wrong with us is that we are now following in a very subtle way of the same trends that we have taken. After Realization – [Open the door.]
所以，我们必须意识到我们出错的地方在于，我们现在以一种非常微妙的方式继续着我们曾经走过的路。在得到自觉之后——[开门。]
After Realization, we think that we are Realized souls, that we have become Yogis now, that we are Realized souls. But one should see that we have become Realized souls, no doubt, but whatever bindings we had before coming to Sahaja Yoga have become subtler. They have not expressed themselves as lights, just we believe that we are Sahaja Yogis. We believe that we have vibrations and also we can feel the vibrations. But still we have not reached that state where we have become the totality. We have to still grow. And if you are settled down with your vibrations and thinking that you are all right, you are sadly mistaken. Whatever were our conditionings before coming to Sahaja Yoga, say you were a hippie, so the enlightenment has come, but then the idea of this love business is there.
在得到自觉之后，我们认为自己已经是开悟的灵魂，已经成为瑜伽士，已经是开悟的灵魂。但我们要认识到，毫无疑问，我们成为了开悟的灵魂，但之前束缚我们的那些东西变得更加微妙了。它们没有像光一样显现出来，我们只是相信自己是霎哈嘉瑜伽士。我们相信自己有生命能量，也能感受到生命能量。但我们还没有达到那种完全的状态。我们还需要成长。如果你满足于目前的生命能量状态，认为自己已经很好了，那你就大错特错了。无论我们在接触霎哈嘉瑜伽之前有什么样的条件反射，比如你是嬉皮士，虽然启蒙已经到来，但那种对爱的执念依然存在。
Supposing you were a business man, so the enlightenment has come to you and you think that Mother should help you in your business, that’s what it is. Supposing now if you were unmarried and you are married in Sahaja Yoga, then you want that your marriage should be successful in Sahaja Yoga. If there has been a problem in your health or something like that, then you have become a subtler person that you think, “I asked help from Mother for that.” But we do not think that what have we contributed to Sahaja Yoga.
假设你是一名商人，启蒙已经降临到你身上，你认为母亲应该在你的生意上帮助你。假如你现在未婚，而在霎哈嘉瑜伽中结婚了，那么你希望你的婚姻在霎哈嘉瑜伽中能够成功。如果之前你有一些健康问题或其他类似的问题，你会成为一个更加细腻的人，心想：“我向母亲求助过这些问题。”但我们却没有想过自己为霎哈嘉瑜伽贡献了什么。
We had certain things which we thought were wrong – maybe that, but in Sahaja Yoga we do not see those things are with us, still continuing with us. To become Virata we have to really examine ourselves, without any anger, malice or anything, but see for ourselves. I know people who are small-minded, who have jealousies and their jealousies continue. Supposing there’s a good musician, they’ll not appreciate if the other person is a musician. He will not appreciate, he will not encourage that person that, “Really, I’m a musician, he’s also a musician, let me help him.”
我们曾认为某些事情是错误的——也许如此，但在霎哈嘉瑜伽中，我们并没有看到这些错误仍然伴随着我们。为了成为Virata（一种更高的意识状态），我们必须真正地自我反省，没有愤怒、恶意或任何负面情绪，而是诚实地面对自己。我知道有些人目光短浅，充满嫉妒，他们的嫉妒心一直存在。假设有一个很好的音乐家，他们不会欣赏其他人的音乐才华。他不会欣赏，也不会鼓励那个人，“我是音乐家，他也是音乐家，让我帮助他吧。”
So the small-mindedness that existed before still becomes in a glorified way. The person will think that “All right, I’m a musician and I sing very well and I’m singing Mother’s praise.” But anybody else who is singing will not be appreciated to that extent as you should expect that you are a part and parcel of the whole. This hand, if it feels hurt, the another hand immediately helps it. In the whole body you see the system is so totally connected. As soon as you eat food, the digestive system starts working. But we should watch ourselves. If you have to become deeper, we have to watch ourselves where are we lacking.
所以，之前存在的小气心态现在变得更为美化了。这个人会认为：“好吧，我是一个音乐家，我唱得很好，我在歌颂母亲。”但其他人唱歌就不会得到同样的欣赏，你应该期望自己是整体的一部分。就像一只手受伤了，另一只手会立刻去帮助它。在整个身体中，系统是如此紧密相连。当你吃东西时，消化系统就开始工作。但我们应该观察自己。如果我们想要更深入，就必须观察自己在哪些方面还欠缺。
Now how does a tree grow? I have told you many times the story that there’s a little cell at the end of the root and it knows, and it knows one purpose: that it has to become a tree, so it goes round. It sees a boulder or something, stone, goes round it, doesn’t fight it. Because ultimately what we have to achieve is to become the tree, so we have to keep that in view, that we have to become the tree and not some sort of a small little frog here and there croaking about Sahaja Yoga. No, that’s not going to work out!
现在，一棵树是如何生长的？我曾经多次给你们讲过这个故事，树根的末端有一个小小的细胞，它知道自己的目标：就是要长成一棵树。因此，它会不断探索。遇到石头或障碍物时，它不会去对抗，而是绕过去。因为最终我们要实现的目标是成为一棵树，所以我们必须时刻牢记这一点：我们的目标是成为一棵树，而不是到处呱噪的小青蛙。不，那样是行不通的！
And this is what I find – also very true about people in, say, England, same thing. The leaders are fighting, the leaders are trying to find out ways and methods how to improve the quality of Sahaja Yogis, but it’s the Sahaja Yogis who have to know that the responsibility is theirs. They have to grow, they have to become Viratas. And this is America, where I told you is the place of Shri Krishna, where Shri Krishna became the Virata.
这也是我发现的情况——在英国也是如此。领导者们在斗争，领导者们在试图找到提高霎哈嘉瑜伽练习者质量的方法，但真正需要认识到责任在于他们自己的是霎哈嘉瑜伽练习者。他们必须成长，他们必须成为Viratas。而这里是美国，我告诉过你们，这里是锡吕•克里希纳的地方，Shri Krishna成为了Virata。
Unless and until you see for yourself that we have to do this, we have to become this… We are still busy with our little, little silly problems. It’s a vicious circle, as soon as you start worrying about your silly problems and your old stuffs still becoming glorified in Sahaja Yoga, then you find the vicious circle starts working, it doesn’t achieve anything, it doesn’t give you good chances, it doesn’t give you confidence. It’s such a thing, it’s like a packaging as they call it.
除非你自己看到我们必须这样做，我们必须成为这样……我们仍然忙于那些小小的问题。这是一个恶性循环，一旦你开始为这些小问题担忧，你的旧习惯在霎哈嘉瑜伽中又被美化，那么你会发现这个恶性循环就开始运作了，它什么都不能成就，它不会给你好的机会，也不会给你信心。这种情况就像是他们所说的包装一样。
But as soon as you decide, “No, I’m going to do it,” – you people are Sahaja Yogis, you know all about Kundalini, you know all about awakening, you know all about higher stages of attainment, you all, every one is capable of giving Realization to people. You know where the thing is stuck, everything you know, but now just think about it, how many of you have been able to do something about it? When people see you their idea about you is very poor. They think that you are stuck up with your wives, so the wife is very dominating, so this is happening. Suddenly you go off. You see your child, worried about the child. You … Sometimes I’m surprised that once they are married, they have a child, then they become nothing but the parents of the child, finished, Sahaja Yoga is finished. Then the child becomes important. But the blessings become the bindings! [Some laughter]
