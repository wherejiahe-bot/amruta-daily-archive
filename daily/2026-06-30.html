No place for cowards

懦夫无处容身
Source: www.amruta.org
◇
Sahaja Yoga is not meant for useless people, is not meant for people who have no courage, is not meant for cowards.
霎哈嘉瑜伽并非为无用之人而设，亦非为缺乏勇气、胆小怯懦者而设。
It’s not meant for people who run after money and who run after all these material things.
它并不适合那些追逐金钱、一味追求物质享受的人。
Especially the first people who are the foundation of Sahaja Yoga, they have to be people of great calibre and of great desire to achieve their state of emancipation, because they are the people who are going to save the whole humanity.
尤其是那些作为萨哈杰瑜伽之根基的首批修行者，他们必须是德才兼备、怀有强烈解脱愿求之人，因为他们正是肩负拯救全人类重任的中坚力量。
They are the redeemers, they are the one who are going to manifest the salvation of this doomed humanity.
他们是救赎者，他们将彰显这注定沉沦的人类的救恩。
Those who are weak can be strengthened because the energy lies within them.
弱者亦可变强，因为能量本就蕴于自身。
Those who are unhealthy can be cured.
身体不健康的人可以治愈。
Those who are mentally disturbed can be brought round.
精神失常者是可以被治愈的。
But there is no place for cowards in this.
但这里容不下懦夫。
You have to be courageous and cheerful people, for one thing, because you represent that Force which is fighting for the Divine, for God Almighty, you are His hands.
首先，你们必须是勇敢而乐观的人，因为你们代表着那股为神圣、为全能的上帝而战的力量，你们就是祂的双手。
And you have to be wise, also.
而且你还得明智。
Stupid people are no good.
愚蠢的人没什么用。
Stupidity can never help you to rise.
愚蠢永远无法助你成功。
You have to be wise people.
你们得是聪明人。
Useless people, those who run after nonsensical things, let them go wherever they like, they’ll come back when their time and turn will come.
无用之人，那些追逐荒唐之事的家伙，随他们去吧，等到了该来的时侯，自然会回来。
We need not be many people for this.
为此，我们不必人多。
Very few can save the world, but they have to be strong and absolutely established in their understanding of the powers of their Spirit, powers of their Father who is Almighty.
能够拯救世界的人寥寥无几，但他们必须坚强，并且在对自身灵性之力、以及那位全能之父所赋予的权能的认识上，达到坚定不移的境界。
Don’t worry what happened to you before getting Realisation.
不要担心你在获得证悟之前所经历的一切。
Those who ponder over these things will not achieve much.
那些沉思于这些事情的人，终究难有大成。
Past is past, is finished now.
过去的事已经过去了，现在都结束了。
Today, you are facing Me and I am facing you.
今天，你正面对着我，我也正面对着你。
This is the present.
这就是礼物。
