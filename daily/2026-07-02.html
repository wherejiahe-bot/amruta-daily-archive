Comforter, counselor, and redeemer

安慰者、Counselor和救赎者
Source: amruta.today
◇
Sahaja Yoga gives you the complete picture of the totality of consciousness.
霎哈嘉瑜伽给你意识整体的完整画面。
As you grow higher and higher in Sahaja Yoga you yourself understand and can work out this great system.
随着你在霎哈嘉瑜伽中成长得越来越高，你自己就能理解并能完成这个伟大的系统。
First, we have to accept that we are not only this human body, nor are we emotions, neither are we ego and conditionings, but we are the Pure Spirit.
首先，我们必须接受我们不仅是这个人体，也不是我们的情绪，也不是我们的自我和条件，但我们是纯粹的精神。
One other thing that we have to understand, that there is an all-pervading power of Love which does all this living work of creating flowers, fruits, and making us human beings.
我们必须了解的另一件事是，有一种普遍存在的爱的力量，它完成了所有这些创造鲜花，水果和使我们成为人类的活生生的工作。
Of course, in science they do not talk of love but even the doctors have to love the patients otherwise they cannot dedicate.
当然，在科学中，他们不谈论爱，但即使是医生也必须爱病人，否则他们就不能奉献。
First the doctor has to become the Spirit.
首先，医生必须成为精神。
He has to feel the cool vibrations on his head which are the fruit of this all-pervading power of Love.
他必须感觉到头上的凉爽振动，这是这种爱的全部力量的果实。
This is the instrument which you have to use, first of all for cleansing yourself and for cleansing others.
这是您必须使用的工具，首先用于清洁自己和清洁他人。
In the medical terminology we can say that the parasympathetic system is looked after by the central channel and the left and right is looked after by two more channels.
在医学术语中，我们可以说副交感神经系统由中央通道照顾，而左和右由另外两个通道照顾。
According to Sahaja Yoga, left and right sympathetic are two separate energies.
根据霎哈嘉瑜伽，左和右交感是两个独立的能量。
The left one is the one which comforts us, the right one is the one which counsels us and the centre one is the one which redeems us.
左边的是安慰我们的人，右边的是劝告我们的人，中间的是救赎我们的人。
All this happens after realization, because you have to have the correction.
所有这一切都发生在实现之后，因为你必须有修正。
