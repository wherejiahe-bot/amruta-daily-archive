Receiving the beauty that is promised

Receiving the beauty that is promised
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
You have to know the meaning of your life because you were created for a purpose – from amoeba to this stage you were brought – and now the One who has created you, the One who has given you all the beauties and the bounties of this universe, wants you to know Him and enjoy all the bounties, like the father bestowing properties, all his enjoyments on this earth, out of his love.
你必须知道你生命的意义，因为你被创造是有目的的 -- 从变形虫到你被带到这个阶段 -- 现在创造你的人，给你这个宇宙所有的美丽和恩惠的人，希望你认识他，享受所有的恩惠，就像父亲赐予财产一样，他在这个地球上的所有享受，都是出于他的爱。
It’s the manifestation of His love that has brought us to this state, and His love only is going to give you that status by which you are going to be enthroned into the kingdom of God.
正是他的爱的表现将我们带到了这个状态，他的爱只会给你那种地位，让你登上上帝的国。
Instead, I know people have been harmed much more.
相反，我知道人们受到的伤害更大。
It’s a very, very simple proposition, which is difficult for human beings to understand, that this was desired as a father desires for his son.
这是一个非常非常简单的命题，人类很难理解，这是父亲对儿子的渴望。
Even when he is not yet a father, he thinks of a son: how he is going to bring him up and how he is going to bestow all his love and all he is earnings on that child.
即使他还不是父亲，他也会想到一个儿子: 他将如何抚养他，以及他将如何将他所有的爱和所有的收入都赋予那个孩子。
In the same manner, the Great Father has planned the whole thing in such a beautiful manner and He is going to look after His own creation.
以同样的方式，伟大的父亲以如此美丽的方式计划了整个事情，他将照顾自己的创造。
No one can destroy it.
没有人能够摧毁它。
He’s Almighty.
他是全能的。
Only thing is that you may destroy yourself, because He has made you free.
唯一的是，你可以摧毁自己，因为他让你自由。
He has given you freedom.
他给了你自由。
He has treated you as a friend.
他把你当作朋友。
Because if you have to be enthroned, you have to be a free person and you must know how to enjoy the glory of freedom.
因为如果你必须登基，你必须是一个自由的人，你必须知道如何享受自由的荣耀。
In that freedom if you have chosen something wrong then you cannot blame God for that.
在这种自由中，如果你选择了错误的东西，那么你就不能为此责怪上帝。
But even if it is just a mistake then He will work it out with a thousand fold of His grace and mercies to see that you are saved.
但即使这只是一个错误，他也会用他的恩典和慈悲千倍来解决这个问题，看看你得救了。
He’s going to use all His powers to see that you receive that beauty that is promised.
他会用他所有的力量来看到你得到承诺的美丽。
That is what is Sahaj Yoga.
这就是瑜伽。
