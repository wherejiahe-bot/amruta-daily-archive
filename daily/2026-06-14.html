Tending to the Self

照料真我
https://amruta.today/ https://amruta.today/
• • •
Supposing a seed evolves into a better fruit — it doesn’t have to do anything about it, somebody else has to do.
假设一粒种子进化成更好的果实——它本身并不需要为此做任何事，而是由别人来完成。
If you sprout a seed then you have to look after it throughout.
如果你让种子发芽，那么你就必须全程照料它：
Sprout the seed then take the seedlings, put them right somewhere then let them grow into a tree, then you have to prune the tree.
先让种子发芽，再将幼苗移植到合适的地方，让它长成一棵树，接着你还得修剪这棵树。
Whatever you have to do, you have to do all the time, you have to look after the tree.
无论需要做什么，你都必须持续不断地照料这棵树。
But after this evolution, this jumping, this happening, when you become the Self, you have to do something about it: you have to.
然而，在这次进化、这次跃升、这次觉醒之后，当你成为真我（Self）时，你就必须有所行动——你非做不可。
Because you become the master of your being.
因为你已成为自己存有的主人，
It is you who has to work it out.
必须由你自己来完成这一切。
In Sahaja Yoga there are many people who get Realisation: it’s very easy.
在霎哈嘉瑜伽中，有许多人获得自觉——这其实非常容易。
The confusion is so great that people want to come out of it, [so] they get it.
正因为困惑如此深重，人们渴望从中解脱，于是便得到了自觉。
But again they are drawn back into it because that time which is so important, the intermediary time, where you have to stick on to that area, to that new dimension of collective consciousness, your willpower doesn’t act, and that is the time you require this will power.
但随后，他们又往往被拉回旧有的状态，因为在那个至关重要的过渡阶段——你必须牢牢安住于那个新维度的集体意识之中——你的意志力却无法发挥作用，而恰恰正是在这个时刻，你最需要这份意志力。
Already you are exhausted.
你早已精疲力竭：
So many are exhausted because of seeking, because of worrying, because of thinking, wrong seeking, gurus, this that.
太多人因不断追寻、忧虑、思虑、错误的求道方式、追随各种导师，以及种种杂念而耗尽心力。
All kinds of exhaustions are there: family life, married life.
各种形式的疲惫充斥着你的生活——家庭生活、婚姻生活，
Every sort of problem that you have faced, you have already exhausted your willpower.
你所经历的每一种困境，早已将你的意志力消耗殆尽。
And when even you get Realised, you have the cup, but you have no strength to lift, within yourself, to raise it to your mouth and to enjoy the nectar of your Realisation.
因此，即便你获得了自觉，手中虽已捧起盛满甘露的杯子，却无力将它举到唇边，无法品尝自觉的甘露。
More so in places that are supposed to be developed.
这种情况在那些所谓“发达”的地方尤为明显。
In developing that part of your systems you have exhausted yourself much more.
在发展你自身系统的过程中，你反而更加透支了自己。
I mean it was all right up to a point, but when human beings know something, they’ll go to the extreme end of everything.
我的意思是，发展到某个程度原本尚可，但人类一旦掌握某种知识，便会走向极端。
Now, if you know electricity, then everything must work on electricity, your lift will work on electricity, this will work on electricity, now computers have come, all right.
现在，如果你懂得电，那么一切就都必须靠电来运作：你的电梯靠电运转，这个靠电，那个也靠电；如今电脑出现了，好吧，没问题。
Now they will make human beings into electrical electrodes!
接下来他们就要把人类变成电极了！
I don’t know what they’re going to make out of that.
我不知道他们最终会搞出什么名堂——
I mean, going to that extent.
我的意思是，事情已经发展到这种地步了。
