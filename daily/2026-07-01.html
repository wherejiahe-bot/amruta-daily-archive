飞速进入上帝的国度

Blasting into the Kingdom of God

1985-07-01

Now as you have seen a spacecraft

你们已见过宇宙飞船的发射过程

in the same way our evolution has taken place.

人类的进化亦复如是。

Spacecraft.

如同种子萌发这一生命过程，

Like any living process of a seed sprouting,

种子之内早已蕴藏了整棵大树的全部潜能；

Like in a spacecraft which is not a living process,

而宇宙飞船虽非生命体，

but has got containers one into another built in,

却也由层层嵌套的容器构成。

No, no physical, emotional, mental and spiritual.

同样，我们的存在也由物质、情感、心智与灵性等多重“容器”层层相套而成——确切地说，是物质、情感、心智与灵性依次内嵌。

So, when one container is thrown into the air which is accelerated by an explosion

当飞船发射时，第一级推进器爆炸加速，

and is travels with a much greater speed

将飞船推至高速；

then another one is again exploded and travels with a much much more speed,

随后第二级再次爆炸，

like that it goes the last where at the spiritual point you become a seeker,

使其速度倍增；

but then it is blasted and triggered into the space.

如此层层推进，直至最后一级在灵性之点引爆，将飞船送入浩瀚太空。

In the same way, our evolution takes place,

人类的进化亦循此道：

we are at a stage where we are to be now triggered into space,

我们如今正处于临界点，即将被“引爆”进入太空——

that is the kingdom of God.

那正是神的国度。

This is a very important time,

此刻至关重要，

I call it a blossom time.

我称之为“绽放之时”。

Like out of one seed the whole tree has developed,

正如一粒种子长成大树，

leaves have come out,

枝叶繁茂，

flowers have come out

鲜花盛开，

and now the flowers have to become the fruits.

如今花朵正待结成果实。
