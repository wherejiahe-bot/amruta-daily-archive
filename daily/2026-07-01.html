Blasting into the Kingdom of God

轰然进入上帝的国度
Source: www.amruta.org
◇
Now as you have seen a spacecraft in the same way our evolution has taken place.
如今，正如你所见，宇宙飞船的出现与我们自身的演化历程如出一辙。
Spacecraft.
航天器。
Like any living process of a seed sprouting, the seed has everything in it which comes out of the seed.
正如种子发芽这一生命过程一样，种子本身便蕴含着日后长成的一切。
Like in a spacecraft which is not a living process, but has got containers one into another built in, thus we have physical, mental, emotional and spiritual containers built into one another.
正如一艘航天器并非生命体，却内置了层层相套的舱室；同样地，我们的身心灵也彼此嵌套、相互交融。
No, no physical, emotional, mental and spiritual.
不，不是身体上的，而是情感、心理和精神层面的。
So, when one container is thrown into the air which is accelerated by an explosion and is travels with a much greater speed then another one is again exploded and travels with a much much more speed, like that it goes the last where at the spiritual point you become a seeker, but then it is blasted and triggered into the space.
于是，当一个集装箱被抛向由爆炸所加速的空中，并以远高于另一个集装箱的速度飞驰时，另一个集装箱又被引爆，速度更上一层楼。如此层层推进，直至最后，在精神层面你已然成为求道者；然而随即，它再次被猛烈炸开，被激射进广袤的宇宙之中。
In the same way, our evolution takes place, we are at a stage where we are to be now triggered into space, that is the kingdom of God.
同样地，我们的进化也在发生；我们正处于这样一个阶段：即将被触发进入那片属于上帝的国度——也就是灵性之境。
This is a very important time, I call it a blossom time.
这是一个非常重要的时期，我称之为花期。
Like out of one seed the whole tree has developed, leaves have come out, flowers have come out and now the flowers have to become the fruits.
正如从一粒种子长成整棵树，叶子抽枝，花朵绽放，而如今，那些花终将结出果实。
First of all, it is very high time for us to give up our mental projections and mental conceptions.
首先，我们早就该放下自己的心理投射与观念执念了。
You cannot conceive God, in human awareness.
在人类的意识中，你无法构想上帝。
You cannot understand Christ, Mohammed, Buddha, anyone. with human awareness, because it is limited.
你无法理解基督、穆罕默德、佛陀，也无法理解任何人。凭借人类的意识，因为它是有限的。
You have to enter into the unlimited awareness, then only you can understand them.
你必须进入无限的觉知，唯有如此，你才能理解他们。
