Modern movies often glamorize destructive tendencies

Source: amruta.today
◇
The second thing that happens to you when you become the Spirit is that you become so powerful that nothing dominates you. Nothing dominates you.
当你成为灵之后，第二件发生的事，就是你变得无比强大——如此强大，以至于没有任何事物能支配你。
There is no habit that can dominate you.
没有任何习气能控制你，
No temptation can dominate you and you become very powerful.
没有任何诱惑能左右你，你变得极其有力。
But at the same time you are extremely compassionate, you are extremely kind, so it is a very compassionate power that you develop of the Spirit and you become a new personality, a wonderful personality.
但与此同时，你又充满极致的慈悲与温柔。因此，你所发展出的，是一种充满慈悲的力量，你成为一个崭新的人格，一个美妙的人格。
We know when we have Christmas, when we have Easter, we give eggs to people.
我们知道，在圣诞节或复活节时，我们会送彩蛋给人。
This denotes the Resurrection of Christ as our own Resurrection, that an egg has to become the bird.
这象征着基督的复活，也象征着我们自身的复活——因为蛋终将化为飞鸟。
It is symbolic of that.
这正是一种隐喻：
That we are like eggs when we are closed, when we have this &amp;#8216;I-ness&amp;#8217; within us, and then suddenly when this happens to us, this egg becomes the bird and you become completely free and you know what freedom is.
当我们封闭自我、内心充满“我执”时，我们就像一枚封闭的蛋；而当自觉降临于我们，这枚蛋便破壳而出，化作飞鸟，你便彻底获得自由，并真正明白何为自由。
Freedom is not a license but a freedom which absolutely does not curb anybody’s freedom nor is [it] giving you any bondages.
真正的自由并非放纵，而是一种既不束缚他人、也不给自己套上枷锁的自由。
It is something so great it should happen to all of us.
这是一种如此伟大的境界，愿我们人人都能亲历此境。
