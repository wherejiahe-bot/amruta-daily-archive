Challenging one&#8217;s past

挑战过去
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
But now you see when you reach a certain state then you should not allow anything of the past to come back.
但如今你们要知道，当你达到某种境界时，就不该再让任何过去的事物卷土重来。
I would even suggest to give a challenge to the past….
我甚至建议你们向过去发出挑战。
I would even suggest to give a challenge to the past….
我甚至建议你们向过去发出挑战。
So, you have to challenge the past….
所以，你们必须挑战过去。
Ah, now let’s see what was the past, “Oh that one was, that one was, all right let’s see that, gone to waste, finished, good for Nothing” – like that.
啊，现在让我们来看看过去是什么样子：“哦，那个嘛，那个嘛……好吧，就看看那个吧，已经白白浪费了，结束了，毫无价值”——诸如此类。
Then you are fixed into this point.
这样一来，你就被牢牢钉在这个点上了。
But even if you are afraid of the past, to look at it, “Oh God”, and you feel funny or you get angry with yourself or you feel upset about it – then yet you have not reached the point.
但即使你害怕回顾过去，一想到它就喊：“哦，天哪！”然后觉得好笑（笑声），或是对自己生气，又或因此感到沮丧——那你就还没有真正到达那个境界。
So now you have to see it that you are at a distance, that’s why you are seeing it.
所以现在你必须看清一点：你与过去之间是有距离的，正因如此，你才能看见它。
Nothing can touch you.
没有任何东西能够再触及你。
So the judgment will come from you to yourself, “Am I still involved in the past that was last, that was yesterday, is finished now? Am I even now bothered about it?”
所以，审判将由你自己对自己作出：“我是否仍纠缠于过去——那个属于昨天、如今已然终结的过去？我现在是否还在为它烦扰？”
And your Mother will also sometimes test you on that.
你们的母亲有时也会在这方面考验你们。（锡吕·玛塔吉笑）
Sometimes I may remind you of some mistake of yours and then see what happens to you.
有时，我或许会提醒你曾犯下的某个错误，然后观察你的反应。
And then I will know where are My children.
这样，我就能知道我的孩子们究竟处于什么状态。（笑声）
Now you have become the fruit, which is ripe.
如今你们已结成果实，而且成熟了。
So why should you worry about things which you are not anymore?
那么，为何还要为那些早已不属于你的事情忧心呢？
Now if you have become a full-fledged bird why should you worry about the egg that used to smell so badly?
既然你已成长为一只羽翼丰满的飞鸟，又何必再去挂念那枚曾经臭气熏天的蛋呢？（笑声）
So this is the attitude one must have towards oneself, the confidence within yourself.
这正是一个人对待自己应有的态度——内心充满自信。
So that’s how you challenge your past.
唯有如此，你才能真正挑战自己的过去，甚至敢于挑战未来。
Now, not only that, try to see, “Suppose this, what can happen next?” (Laughter) This is the way the Western people think, very common.
现在，不妨试着去设想一下：“假设如此，接下来可能会发生什么？”（笑声）这恰恰是西方人极其普遍的思维方式。
Indeed, if you ask someone, “Please now go and call that person”, he will immediately say, “He may not be in.” (Laughter) “Let me see what is the time – at this time he should have gone out, so he must have gone out.” (Laughter)
的确如此，如果你请某人：“麻烦你现在去给那个人打个电话”，对方立刻就会说：“他可能不在啊。”（笑声）“让我先看看现在几点——这个时间他应该出门了，所以他肯定已经出去了。”（笑声）
Why don’t you just dial the number and see? And he is there.
可你为何不直接拨通电话呢？结果一打，那人就在那儿。
This is the way the Western people think, very common.
这正是西方人凡事都要预先设想未来的典型作风。
