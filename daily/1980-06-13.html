Value through testing

通过测试获得价值

1980-06-13

Once you get the experience [of kundalini awakening] then you must know what that power is.

一旦你获得了这种体验[即昆达里尼觉醒的体验]，你就必须知道这股力量是什么。

Then you must use it.

然后你必须使用它。

Like, one gentleman came to see me, he’s a big writer.

比如，有一位先生来见我，他是一位大作家。

He said “Yes Mother, I came to you, I had the cool breeze in the hand.

他说：“是的，母亲，我来见您，我在手上感觉到了凉风。

I did feel better, I felt relaxed”, but nothing!

我感觉好多了，我感到放松了”，但仅此而已！

“I went home, I started thinking about it, I lost my cool breeze.”

“我回到家，我开始思考这件事，我失去了那股凉风。”

That’s all!

就这样！

No transformation!

没有任何转变！

I said “It is like, I gave you a diamond and you don’t know what diamond is!

我说：“这就像，我给你一颗钻石，而你却不知道什么是钻石！

You go and put it somewhere.

你把它放在某个地方，

Cover it with something.

用东西盖住它。

There’s no light coming in.”

没有光线透进来。”

You have to go and ask others.

你必须去询问他人。

You have to test it.

你必须去测试它。

You have to understand Kundalini,

你必须理解昆达里尼，

you have to raise it in others.

你必须让它在他人体内升起。

You have to value it by testing it.

你必须通过测试来珍视它。
