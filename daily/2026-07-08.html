Zen Tea Ceremony and Guru Principle

禅茶仪式与上师原则
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
So, the eating of good food is a kind of a addiction.
所以，要吃好的食物是一种嗜好。
It’s a kind of an addiction; it’s something like drug – that you must have good food.
它是一种瘾，就像吸毒 — 你一定要吃好的食物。
So Zen started this system of what they call the tea ceremony.
因此禅开展了他们称为茶道的制度。
In the tea ceremony what they did, I mean, I have been through that and it’s really quite a test of people.
在茶道里，他们做什么，我是说我曾经体验，这真的对人是种考验。
With Me it was alright, but the rest of them got really frightened.
对我还可以，但却吓怕其他人。
That in the tea ceremony whatever rituals they do they give you a tea, which is so bitter,
在茶道里，不管有什么仪式，他们都给你茶，那些茶是很苦的，
and we can say [like] quinine raised to power 108! It’s like that.
我们可以说像奎宁，提升至力量108，就像这样。
And they give you very nicely, handle you with such ritual that you have to take it.
他们很友善地给你，以这种仪式对待你，你必须接受。
So first they say, “You see the cup without thinking.”
他们先说：「看着杯子，不要思想。」
So he tried to make thoughtless awareness through the cup.
他想透过杯子令你有无思无虑的觉醒状态。
I don’t know how much one can achieve with that.
我不知道有多少人能做到。
But that tea if you take once, your tongue is good for anything.
但这种茶，你只要喝一口，你的舌头就不好受。
And to compensate it they give you something sweet, which is again sweetness raised to power 108,
为补偿你的不好受，他们给你一些甜点，再次很甜的提升至力量108，
so sweet that it becomes bitter! So sweet.
这种甜味甜得变苦！那么甜。
So this is just to conquer the tongue
这样做只为征服舌头。
[that] Zen must have thought that better give them this tea ceremony where there is no tea,
禅必定想最好给他们这种没有茶的茶道，
there’s nothing but just you really give such shocks to your tongue,
只为令你的舌头真的感受到这种震撼，
so that after that you can eat whatever you get.
因此，在此之后，你能吃任何你拿到的食物。
And that’s why you must know that Japanese can eat anything.
这就是为何你必定知道，日本人什么都吃。
They have solved their food problem because they can eat anything!
他们已解决他们食物短缺的问题，因为他们什么也能吃！
They can just fish out some crabs or could be some prawns,
他们只要钓一些蟹或一些虾，
just peel them out like peanuts and eat them like that.
把牠们像花生般剥掉壳，再吃掉。
I think Zen has solved this problem for them, because they have food shortage
我想禅已为他们解决这问题，因为他们有食物短缺的问题，
and this is how they can eat everything:
他们就是这样能什么都吃：
they can eat the bark of the tree, they can eat the snake, they can eat the lizards, they can eat the frogs, everything they can eat,
他们能吃树皮，吃蛇，吃蜥蜴，吃青蛙，什么都能吃，
so all their eating problems are solved in that country by Zen, I think, because you have no taste.
所以禅解决了这个国家吃的问题，我想，因为你已经没有味觉。
