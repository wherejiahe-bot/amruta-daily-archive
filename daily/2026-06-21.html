No need for fear or malice

不需要恐惧或恶意
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
There’s a saying in Sanskrit ‘Vinaasha kale vipreet buddhi’ means, ‘Their destruction will start by their own stupid methods.’ [Those who will try to destroy you] will become stupid.
梵语中有一句话 “vinaasha kale vipreet buddhi'” 的意思是，“他们的毁灭将以他们自己愚蠢的方法开始。” [那些试图摧毁你的人] 将变得愚蠢。
And at every stage, you’ll see they are becoming stupid and by their stupidity they are destroying themselves, you don’t have to worry about that part.
在每一个阶段，你都会看到他们变得愚蠢，他们的愚蠢正在摧毁自己，你不必担心这一部分。
So that’s why you don’t need any destructive powers.
所以这就是为什么你不需要任何破坏力。
It will all be done by this All-pervading Divine power.
这一切都将由这种普遍的神圣力量来完成。
So that part you have to give up.
所以你必须放弃那部分。
You just forgive that person.
你只要原谅那个人。
As soon as you forgive, the responsibility from you shifts and then Kundalini rises.
一旦你原谅，你的责任就会转移，然后昆达里尼就会上升。
You don’t have to carry any malice, anything against him but just laugh because they are blind, because they are stupid, they are foolish.
你不必对他有任何恶意，任何反对他的事情，只要笑，因为他们是盲目的，因为他们是愚蠢的，他们是愚蠢的。
What do we do if you see an idiot.
如果你看到一个白痴，我们该怎么办？
We may not laugh in his presence but we do, isn’t it, at his back because he’s an idiot.
我们可能不会在他面前笑，但我们会在他背后笑，不是吗，因为他是个白痴。
So they become idiotic and you see them becoming idiotic and then you just start, you see that’s the humour of Sahaja Yoga.
所以他们变得愚蠢，你看到他们变得愚蠢，然后你开始，你看到这就是霎哈嘉瑜伽的幽默。
You have to have some humour also isn’t it?
你必须有一些幽默，不是吗？
And then you start seeing these jokers, you see, behaving in that manner and the whole circus they do is nothing but jocular.
然后你开始看到这些小丑，你看，以这种方式行事，他们所做的整个马戏团只不过是开玩笑。
So there’s nothing to have any fear from any human being; same about organizations or anything.
因此，任何人都没有任何恐惧; 组织或任何东西都一样。
Naturally you have to know one thing: that as you are Realized souls, you have seen the truth, you are in the light so they are going to oppose you, they are going to oppose you.
自然地，你必须知道一件事: 当你意识到灵魂，你已经看到了真相，你在光中，所以他们会反对你，他们会反对你。
They did this, isn’t it, to others; everybody suffered so much.
他们这样做了，不是吗，对其他人来说; 每个人都遭受了如此多的痛苦。
But now no more sufferings to you, only thing you can enjoy the humor.
但现在没有更多的痛苦给你，只有你可以享受幽默。
Just understand this point, that nothing will destroy you, no one can touch you but they are there just to supply some humor for you.
只要明白这一点，没有什么会毁了你，没有人可以碰你，但他们只是为你提供一些幽默。
If you take such a light attitude towards them, all this fear and left side will go away.
如果你对他们采取这样一个轻的态度，所有这些恐惧和左侧将消失。
What are these baddhas, and what are these bhoots, and what are these tantrikas?
这些baddhas是什么，这些bhoots是什么，这些tantrikas是什么？
You are so powerful.
你太强大了。
Just with one glance you can see them dancing on their heads, the other way round.
只要看一眼，你就能看到他们在头上跳舞，反之。
As long as you’ll have fear Kundalini won’t rise because She doesn’t rise for people who are cowards.
只要你害怕昆达里尼不会崛起，因为她不会为懦夫崛起。
Cowardice She is not going to support.
她不会支持的懦弱。
If you are a coward She said, “All right”.
如果你是个懦夫，她说: “好吧。
You are walking on the street, normally people are afraid if there’s darkness, afraid that somebody might attack, but a Sahaja Yogi does not, if he’s a real Sahaja Yogi, because he knows there are ganas and there are angels around him.
你走在街上，通常人们会害怕黑暗，害怕有人会攻击，但是一个霎哈嘉瑜伽士不会，如果他是一个真正的霎哈嘉瑜伽士，因为他知道有加纳人，有天使在他周围。
No one can touch him, and the one who will try, these ganas will make such a fool out of that person that you’ll have a nice chance to laugh at him.
没有人可以碰他，而那些会尝试的人，这些ganas会让那个人变得如此愚蠢，以至于您将有很好的机会嘲笑他。
Now you watch and see.
现在你看看。
So this left side or this fear should go.
所以这个左边或者这个恐惧应该消失。
Once this fear will go all your slyness, all your intrigues, all your jealousies will just disappear.
一旦这种恐惧将消除您所有的狡猾，所有的阴谋，所有的嫉妒就会消失。
That’s how the Kundalini will rise very well.
这就是昆达里尼会很好地崛起的方式。
