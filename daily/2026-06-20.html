Divine Love

那是你必须达到的心境
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
It is the state of mind one has to achieve.
那是你必须达到的心境。
There you do not judge anyone, you don’t judge anyone.
在那里，你不评判任何人，你不评判任何人。
You know there is a defect, but you envelope it with your love, make a beautiful pearl out of that.
你知道有一个缺陷，但你用你的爱将它包裹起来，从中制作出一颗美丽的珍珠。
Like the sun shine that is spreading everywhere, it does not see any defects and count the leaves, it just gives and gives and gives.
就像洒向各地的阳光，它看不到任何缺陷，也不数叶子，它只是给予，给予，再给予。
That’s how one has to achieve that state where you just give without consideration of my-my or material considerations and other nonsensical gross methods of approaching it.
这就是你必须达到的那种状态，在这种状态下，你只是给予，而不考虑“我的”或物质上的考量，以及其他荒谬粗俗的接近方法。
Allow your heart to melt it, allow love to slip and you are enjoying it, nobody else, you really enjoy it.
让你的心融化它，让爱滑入其中，你正在享受它，不是别人，你真的在享受它。
If others get angry with you so what, if they say anything nonsensical, so what, you are not enjoying them, you are enjoying yourself.
如果其他人对你生气，那又怎样？如果他们说了任何荒谬的话，那又怎样？你不是在享受他们，你是在享受你自己。
This is real love and not a farce.
这才是真爱，而不是闹剧。
For love I have to write books.
对于爱，我必须写书。
If all the oceans become the ink and the sky becomes paper, I cannot write.
如果所有的海洋变成墨水，天空变成纸张，我也写不完。
It is so beautiful, it is so energy giving, it is so radiant.
它是如此美丽，如此赋予能量，如此光辉灿烂。
I cannot tell you how many words one can have for that.
我无法告诉你可以用多少词来形容它。
You achieve that love, that ‘s real love not the way human beings describe it.
你实现了那种爱，那就是真爱，而不是人类描述的那种方式。
It gives forgiveness that you enjoy.
它给予你享受的宽恕。
It gives complete confidence; it gives you complete self respect and respect of another person.
它给予完全的自信；它给予你完全的自尊和对别人的尊重。
It is the communication of the Spirit with others, it’s not rational, it may not be logical.
它是灵与他人之间的交流，它不是理性的，可能也不合乎逻辑。
We cannot explain how God grace for it.
我们无法解释上帝是如何赐予它的恩典的。
For example an archbishop of black magic gets Realization.
例如，一个黑魔法的大主教获得了证悟。
I am [?] enamored, I am enamored by the grace.
我被[?]迷住了，我被恩典迷住了。
Those who were going to hell are given the beauty of the Self.
那些原本要下地狱的人被赋予了自我的美丽。
Can you describe it in human words, you cannot.
你能用人话描述它吗？你不能。
This is love.
这就是爱。
