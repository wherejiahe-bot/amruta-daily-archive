Avoiding plastic

避免使用塑料
https://www.amruta.org/1982/06/07/ego-and-humility-london-1982/
◇
English
But just see you are spiritual.
中文
但你只需看看，你是有灵性的。
English
You will find it impossible to use plastics after some time, because you will know that the plastics give you horrid vibrations.
中文
过一段时间，你会发现自己根本无法使用塑料，因为你会知道塑料会给你带来可怕的生命能量干扰。
English
After some time you will really find it repulsive.
中文
再过一段时间，你真的会对它感到厌恶。
English
It's all right to sit in a train for a while on a plastic coach, all right, but, to sit in your house on a plastic all the time would be horrible and to wear a plastic coat all the time, our body will refuse.
中文
在火车上短暂地坐在塑料座椅上是没问题的，但如果整天坐在家里的塑料上、整天穿着塑料外套，我们的身体会本能地拒绝。
English
So when people will be spiritually awakened, they'll give up all these nonsensical things.
中文
所以，当人们在灵性上觉醒之后，他们就会放弃所有这些荒唐的东西。
English
So, they may say then, "What will happen to the machines?"
中文
那么，他们可能会问：“机器怎么办？”
English
Now, machines are for us; we are not for the machines.
中文
机器是为我们服务的，我们不是为机器而活。
English
This is another concept is: whatever is created is for us and we are not for that.
中文
这是另一个理念：所有被创造出来的东西都是为我们服务的，而不是反过来。
English
All right, doesn't matter.
中文
好的，没关系。
English
Nothing will go wrong with the machines.
中文
机器不会出什么问题的。
English
Machines should be used for anything that is a public work.
中文
机器应当用于一切公共事务。
English
Like for your motor cars, for your trains, trams, all public work which is outside.
中文
比如汽车、火车、有轨电车，所有户外的公共工程。
English
For houses at the most you can use machines.
中文
在家里，最多可以使用机器。
English
But, for personal things, you must use handmade things, handmade things.
中文
但对于个人物品，你必须使用手工制品，手工制品。
English
For spiritual people, you like to wear something that is handmade or real.
中文
对于有灵性的人来说，你会喜欢穿戴手工制作或天然的东西。
English
I can't wear a nylon sari.
中文
我无法穿尼龙纱丽。
English
Not that I am very fussy about things, but I just can't wear.
中文
不是说我对事物很挑剔，我就是穿不了。
English
After some time, if I do not take out My socks, which are only nylon, what to do?
中文
过一段时间，如果我不脱掉我的袜子——它们只是尼龙的——该怎么办？
English
My vibrations get jammed, completely.
中文
我的生命能量会完全受阻。
English
So, you won't be able to wear next to your skin, near your skin, near your body, eating your food, everything in plastics, cooking in plastics.
中文
所以，你将无法把塑料贴身穿戴、接触皮肤、靠近身体，也无法用塑料盛放食物、用塑料烹饪。
English
I mean it's too much.
中文
我是说，这太过分了。
English
Thank God, cooking I don't think is possible, isn't it?
中文
感谢上天，用塑料烹饪我想是不可能的，不是吗？
English
But aluminium is another thing.
中文
但铝又是另一回事了。
English
Is very bad.
中文
非常不好。
English
Aluminium – you won't be able to use.
中文
铝——你将无法使用。
English
You'll either try copper or brass, I think so.
中文
我想，你们会改用铜或黄铜。
English
Now, handmade things we may have, because they are made by hand, so they have vibrations.
中文
手工制品我们可以拥有，因为它们由双手制成，所以蕴含着生命能量。
English
People would like to have handmade.
中文
人们会喜欢手工制品的。
English
It is a part, so by that we remove the disparity of the world.
中文
这是其中一部分，通过这样做，我们消除了世界上的不平等。
