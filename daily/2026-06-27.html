Self Knowledge

自我认知
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
The meaning of freedom is to know the mechanism of “thy self”, (swa ka tantra).
而“自由”的真正含义，就是了解“自我”的机制（swa ka tantra），
That is, to know the mechanism of the spirit, is the true freedom.
也就是了解灵性的机制，这才是真正的自由。
Without that what is freedom?&amp;#8230;
若没有这一点，自由又算什么呢？
So, I was talking to you about “swa dharma”.
所以，我刚才在跟你们谈“自性法”（swa dharma）。
Swa means “you yourself”.
“Swa”指的是“你，你的真我”。
But you don’t know what you are?
但你真的知道自己是谁吗？
You know your exterior.
你只了解自己的外在，
But, You do not know yourself from inside.
却并不了解内在的自己。
That is why your spirit should be awakened.
正因如此，你的灵性必须被唤醒，
Your spirit should come to your attention.
你的灵性必须进入你的觉知之中。
You will know yourself only when that happens.
唯有到那时，你才能真正认识你自己。
When you don’t know yourself then how do you say “I do this, I do that. Who’s this I?
当你还不认识自己的时候，又怎能说“我做这个，我做那个”？这个“我”究竟是谁？
You should know first who is this “I”.
你首先必须弄清楚这个“我”到底是谁。
And when you realize who this “I” is, then it is so astonishing to find that, like a drop in the ocean, we also vanish in the ocean of love.
而一旦你真正领悟了这个“我”是谁，便会惊奇地发现：我们就像一滴水融入大海一般，也消融在爱的海洋之中。
And that ocean, that all pervading power of love gives us everything.
而那片海洋——那无所不在的爱的力量——会赐予我们一切。
All our ambitions are fulfilled, and all our questions are answered.
我们所有的愿望都会实现，所有的问题都会得到解答。
Only these vibrations have the power to do this.
唯有这些振动拥有如此力量。
We do not have the power to do this, we can only become mad trying to find a solution.
我们自身并没有这样的能力，若试图凭自己的力量去寻找答案，只会陷入疯狂。
Everything becomes all right by this vibrations.
唯有依靠这些振动，一切才会归于圆满。
