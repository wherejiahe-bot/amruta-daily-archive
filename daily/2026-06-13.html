Value through testing

通过测试创造价值
Source: www.amruta.org
◇
Once you get the experience [of kundalini awakening] then you must know what that power is.
一旦你获得了（昆达里尼觉醒的）体验，你就必须明白那股力量究竟是什么。
Then you must use it.
那你就得用它。
Like, one gentleman came to see me, he’s a big writer.
比如，有一位先生来看我，他是一位著名作家。
He said “Yes Mother, I came to you, I had the cool breeze in the hand.
他说：“是的，母亲，我来找您了，手里还握着那阵清凉的微风。”
I did feel better, I felt relaxed”, but nothing! “I went home, I started thinking about it, I lost my cool breeze.” That’s all!
“我确实感觉好些了，也放松了不少”，可还是什么都没有！“我回到家，开始琢磨这事，结果连那阵凉风都没了。”就这些！
No transformation!
没有变化！
I said “It is like, I gave you a diamond and you don’t know what diamond is!
我说：“这就像是，我给了你一颗钻石，而你却连钻石是什么都不知道！”
You go and put it somewhere.
你去把它放在某个地方吧。
Cover it with something.
用什么东西把它盖上。
There’s no light coming in.” You have to go and ask others.
“没有光透进来。”你得去问问别人。
You have to test it.
你得测试一下。
You have to understand Kundalini, you have to raise it in others.
你必须理解昆达里尼，也必须帮助他人唤醒它。
You have to value it by testing it.
你必须通过测试来评估它的价值。
