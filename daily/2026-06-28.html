Antecedents to Sahaja Yoga

Sahaja瑜伽的渊源
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
In the center we had another great movement which is called as Nath, Nathpanthis.
但在中间，我们还有另一场伟大的运动，被称为“纳特”（Nath）或“纳特潘提”（Nath Panthis）。
They say it came from Shiva, but whatever it is; it came from Adinath and these people and got diverted into many types.
他们说这一传承源自湿婆（Shiva）。但不管怎样，它起源于阿迪纳特（Adinath），随后这群人又分化出许多支派。
But those who remained in the middle, one of them was we should say was called Gahininath and his disciple was Nivritinath the elder brother of Gyanadeva.
而那些始终坚守中道的人当中，有一位我们可以说名叫加希尼·纳特（Gahini Nath），他的弟子是尼弗鲁提·纳特（Nivrutti Nath），也就是占那德瓦（Jnanadeva）的兄长。
It is amazing if you read anything about them they know all about chakras, they know all about these all three channels.
令人惊叹的是，如果你读过任何关于他们的记载，就会发现他们对所有脉轮（chakras）了如指掌，对这三条能量通道也全都清楚明白。
Everything they knew thousands of years back.
早在数千年前，他们就已经掌握了这一切。
Then somehow or other they didn’t tell people.
然而，不知为何，他们却没有将这些知识告诉大众，
They kept the knowledge to themselves.
而是将它保留在自己内部。
I can’t say why they did it but they did.
我无法说明他们为何这样做，但他们的确这么做了。
Ultimately, Gyanadeva took permission of his Guru Nivritinath and said that let me tell you, at least let me tell others, you allow me because people should know what works it out.
最终，占那德瓦（Gyanadeva）获得了他的导师尼弗鲁提·纳特（Nivrutti Nath）的许可，并说道：“让我告诉你吧，至少让我告诉其他人，请允许我这样做，因为人们应当知道究竟是什么才能真正奏效。”
So he wrote about Kundalini.
于是，他写下了关于灵量（Kundalini）的内容。
But if you read these ancient books, you will be amazed that the whole thing was described in those books how you get your realization and how then you have those centers which are to be enlightened, what problems come in.
但如果你阅读这些古代典籍，一定会感到惊讶——书中完整描述了你如何获得开悟，以及之后如何照亮那些中心（centers），还会遇到哪些问题。
But the method they employed for achieving it was really hazardous.
然而，他们所采用的达成方法却极其危险。
I don’t know why they had to do it but they did it.
我不知道他们为何非得那样做，但他们的确这么做了。
They were all unmarried people and very strict with themselves and they had to lead a life very secluded from the society.
他们全都保持独身，对自己极为严苛，并且必须过一种与社会完全隔绝的隐居生活。
This was really a very difficult time of ascent they had.
那的确是一段极其艰难的上升历程。
When I read about them I really feel very extremely sad that all these great people who were seeking had to go through such horrible strenuous turmoil for days together and then some of them got realisation.
当我读到他们的经历时，内心真的感到极度悲伤——所有这些伟大的求道者，竟不得不日复一日地经历如此可怕而艰辛的动荡，最终只有其中一些人获得了开悟。
But you are lucky people I should say to be born at this time.
但我要说，你们是幸运的一群人，生在这个时代。
This is a special time, very special time, which is described in all the books, all the scriptures as the last judgment.
这是一个特殊的时代，一个极其特殊的时代，所有典籍、所有经典都将其描述为“最后的审判”。
Now, if you start deciding about it through your mental capacity you cannot achieve anything, because you have only two agencies: one is your conditioning, another is your ego.
现在，如果你试图仅凭自己的心智能力去判断这一切，你将一无所获，因为你只有两种机制：一是你的制约（conditioning），二是你的自我（ego）。
So one has to rely on the natural gifts that we have, is that of this Kundalini and she is your individual mother; she does not want to torture you, she does not want in any way to give you any pains, whatsoever.
因此，你必须依靠我们与生俱来的自然恩赐，那就是这条灵量（Kundalini），而她正是你个体的母亲；她并不想折磨你，也绝不会以任何方式给你带来丝毫痛苦。
