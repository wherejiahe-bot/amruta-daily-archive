Jun 9, 1980

Curing with a purpose

带着目的去治疗
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
We have to give up our gross understanding of Kundalini.
我们必须放弃对灵量的粗浅理解。
Like you would see in Sahaja Yoga when you will be coming, you have seen it all your life — and you will see it more — that so many of them just come to me for getting all right in health, only for that.
就像你们来到霎哈嘉瑜伽中会看到的——你们一生都见过这种情况，将来还会见到更多——那么多人来找我，只是为了让身体好起来，仅此而已。
"I have got a back pain.
「我背痛。
" "I have got a stomach trouble.
」「我胃不好。
" "I have got arthritis," or, "I have got diabetes.
」「我有关节炎，」或「我有糖尿病。
" There's nothing that is not there, and "I am sure you will cure me!"
」什么病都有，而且「我确信您会治好我！」
It's quite a queer situation!
这真是个奇怪的局面！
Because the request that I should cure is all right, but what is the right for this request?
要求我来治疗是可以的，但凭什么有这个要求呢？
Why do you want that Divine should cure you?
为何你觉得上天应当治愈你？
For what?
为了什么？
For what purpose?
目的是什么？
If Divine has to only cure people, It would open lots of hospitals and get some Nobel Prize or something.
若上天只是治病，祂大可开很多家医院，再拿个诺贝尔奖之类的。
But it is not the desire of Divine to go on curing people.
但不断治病并不是上天的意愿。
It would be stupid to just cure people who are good for nothing, useless, absolutely useless, thoroughly wasted.
只是去治疗那些毫无建树、毫无用处、彻底虚度的人，是愚蠢的。
Why should It work [like this]?
祂为何要如此行事？
It has intelligence.
祂有理智。
It has wisdom.
祂有智慧。
Is It going to waste Its energy on curing people who are good for nothing, useless?
难道要把精力浪费在治疗那些一无是处、毫无用处的人身上吗？
Are you going to clean this lamp if you cannot put any light in it?
若一盏灯放不进任何光，你还会去擦亮它吗？
If you have no utility, in the Divine sense, why should Divine cure you?
若你对上天而言毫无用处，上天为何要治愈你？
So all those who believe, "Oh Mother, you must cure people," and that you should cure people, must know that all this is the work of Kundalini when She rises: She nourishes you, She cleanses you, because in this being of yours, God Himself is going to manifest, because this temple of your body is to be cleansed, because God has to enter into it.
所以那些相信"噢母亲，您必须治病"的人，必须知道：这一切都是灵量升起时所做的工作——她滋养你，洁净你，因为在你的存有之中，上帝自己要彰显；因为你这身体的殿堂需要被洁净，因为上帝要进入其中。
That's why the Divine respects and cleanses you and helps you.
这就是为何上天尊重你、洁净你、帮助你。
Even it may help yours, means say a mother is there and her child is not all right, she's worried, so the Divine may cure the child, just to give peace to the mother who is going to manifest God's powers.
祂甚至可能帮助你身边的人——比如一位母亲在那里，她的孩子不好，她很担忧，上天或许会治愈那个孩子，只是为了给那位将要彰显神之力量的母亲以安慰。
Because God's power is the integrated power of all the powers.
因为神的力量是一切力量的总和。
