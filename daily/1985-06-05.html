In the illusion of "doing"

在「做」的幻象之中
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
So after balancing, ascension is needed in the attention. Without that ascent, our attention is at that level where we are not yet superhuman beings, where we have to become non-being.
平衡之后，注意力需要提升。没有这种提升，注意力便停留在尚未成为超人类的层面——在那里，我们要成为"无为"（空无）。
Now the concept of non-being is absolutely Eastern concept, that you become the non-being. So now we are the being and then we become the non-being.
"无为"的概念完全是东方的概念——你成为无为。我们现在是存在，然后要成为无为。
In the state when we are the being what happens to us that we are actually believing in the illusion that we are doing something.
在我们处于"存在"状态时，发生在我们身上的是——我们相信自己正在做事，这是一种幻象。
Because the two dimensions of the left and right give you a feeling that we are doing something because we can go to the right and we can go to the left.
因为左右两个维度给你一种感觉，觉得自己在做事——因为我们可以偏右，也可以偏左。
Like if you want to think you are in love with somebody and imaginary you see…all these things we can build up the castle that side. If you want to think that we have to do something so we can change matter.
比如你想着自己爱上了某人——你可以凭空在那边建起一座想象的城堡。又或者你想着：我们可以做点什么来改变物质。
And how do we change matter is that if something is dead we change the form and we think that some dead…from the dead we have done….so we have done a great job. That means we think we are the doers.
我们改变物质的方式，是将死物改变形态，然后以为从死物中创造了什么——做了一项伟大的工作。这意味着我们以为自己是做事者。
Actually, by changing the forms we have achieved nothing because you cannot create anything. So one must know the creator is beyond us. But we also don't know the creator.
实际上，改变形态我们什么也没有成就，因为你无法真正创造任何东西。所以必须知道，创造者超然于我们之上。然而我们也不认识这个创造者。
This is the dimension that is missing in us. So we live in the three dimensions but if you have to rise to the fourth dimension where we have to know that the doer is somebody else, the creator is somebody else and that His all-pervading power is flowing through you, we will not believe into anything which is the truth.
这正是我们内在缺失的那个维度。我们活在三维之中，但若要提升到第四维度——在那里你必须知道，做事者另有其人，创造者另有其人，祂遍及一切的力量正流经你——我们却不相信任何真实的东西。
