Our attention must go inside

我们的注意力必须转向内在
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
Our evolution has come through our attention fixed outside.
我们的进化历程源于注意力向外执著。
The happening has to go inside.
真正的觉悟必须发生在内在。
But the evolution so far has been by going outside.
然而，迄今为止的进化都是通过向外追求实现的。
Like some fishes felt the land and tried to crawl out
有些鱼感受到陆地的存在，尝试爬上岸
and they became the reptiles.
最终演化成了爬行动物。
The reptiles wanted to run fast
爬行动物渴望跑得更快
so they developed four legs as a tortoise.
于是像乌龟一样长出了四条腿。
As animals they could not eat;
作为动物，它们无法进食
so they raised their head.
于是抬起了头。
Things at the height they could not see, so they raised their body more.
当事物达到他们无法看见的高度时，他们便更加努力地提升自己的身体。
So all the evolution so far has come through the urge of seeking only, but it has been outside.
到目前为止，所有的进化都源于这种追寻的冲动，但这种追寻一直都是外在的。
We have been seeking food, as human beings we are seeking power, we have been seeking money, possessions, comfort.
我们追寻食物，作为人类，我们追寻权力，我们追寻金钱、财物和舒适。
But now the seeking has to go within.
但现在，追寻必须转向内在。
For example, when we are building a house we build all the outside and everything properly.
例如，当我们建造房屋时，我们会先把外部和所有结构都建造得恰到好处。
When it is all done outside then you go inside and start decorating.
当外部一切完工后，你才会进入内部开始装饰。
In the same way you have to go inside to achieve the last breakthrough.
同样地，你必须深入内在，才能实现最后的突破。
For example, we have made a fortress for ourselves and we have to enter inside the fortress to enjoy it.
例如，我们为自己建造了一座堡垒，而我们必须进入堡垒内部才能享受它。
We have to penetrate inside the house to enjoy it.
我们必须深入房屋内部才能享受它。
But our attention is outside.
但我们的注意力却在外在。
We cannot go inside..
我们无法进入内在。
