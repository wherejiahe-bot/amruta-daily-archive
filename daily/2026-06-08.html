A second stone age

第二个石器时代
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
Because the world is so fragmented, so cut off, that everybody feels lonely.
因为这个世界如此支离破碎、如此割裂，每个人都感到孤独。
But that does not, by any chance, mean you should be arrogant to others, you should be fighting with others – no!
但这绝不意味着你应该对他人傲慢，或与人争斗——绝不！
But in darkness a light shines.
但黑暗之中，有一束光在闪耀。
The whole is darkness.
整体是黑暗的。
If the light accepts the darkness, then it becomes darkness!
如果光接受了黑暗，那它也会变成黑暗！
So the light must accept that: 'I am the light, and I am the path,' and should stand up.
所以光必须承认："我是光，我是道路"，并且站稳立场。
That does not in any way mean any arrogance to others, any showing-off to others, but it is the Light.
这并不意味着对他人有任何傲慢或炫耀，但它确实是光。
And the Light, by itself, emits.
光本身就发光。
When this happens to you, you will not hate yourself first of all, but you will love and respect yourself and respect the virtues in you and not your grossness.
当这发生在你身上时，你首先不会厌恶自己，而是会爱自己、尊重自己，尊重你内在的美德，而非你的粗俗。
Kundalini Itself makes you subtler and subtler.
昆达里尼本身会让你越来越精微。
But if you are a stone what can Kundalini do?
但若你是一块石头，昆达里尼又能如何？
Now it is for you to see where you are stone.
现在你要看清自己哪里像石头。
There should be a lot of heart searching.
应当有许多内省。
The greatest stone-age is nowadays (laughing).
当今时代可以说是最大的石器时代。（笑声）
Heart is like a stone!
心就像一块石头！
It doesn't move.
它一动不动。
When talking to others, it doesn't move.
跟人交谈时，它也不动。
It has no ripple in it, there's no joy in it.
其中没有涟漪，没有喜悦。
It's like a stone sitting there to hit everyone.
它就像一块坐在那里等着打击所有人的石头。
You see anybody sees you, 'Oh Bala! He's coming! Go from the other road!' 'Who?' 'That fellow with the stone heart!'
你看，任何人看见你都会说："哦，Bala！他来了！走另一条路！""谁？""那个铁石心肠的家伙！"
And you feel you are all right, you are a very good person because you are stone hearted.
而你却觉得自己很好，因为你心如磐石。
You think you can dominate the whole world because you are stone hearted, you can give psychological treatments to others, keep quiet, don't talk to anyone, shout at others and do whatever you please, because you have a stone heart.
你以为可以主宰整个世界，因为你心如磐石；你可以给别人做心理辅导，保持沉默，不与任何人交谈，对人喊叫，随心所欲——因为你有一颗石头心。
Nothing happens to your stone there.
你那块石头心纹丝不动。
Now this stone heartedness is the sign that you should not be any more in Sahaj Yog.
而这心硬如石，正是不应再留在霎哈嘉瑜伽中的信号。
You must have a heart as your Mother has: pulsating with love, with compassion, with joy, with happiness, with giving.
你应当拥有一颗像你的母亲那样的心：跳动着爱、慈悲、喜悦、幸福与给予。
That should be the attitude, not rationality, nothing.
这才是应有的态度，而不是理性，什么都不是。
It is the feeling: feeling for the pains of others, feeling for their longings and feeling for their aspirations.
是感受：感受他人的痛苦，感受他们的渴望，感受他们的志向。
Just feeling them within yourself their desire to be the Whole, to be the Ocean Itself, itself is so fulfilling.
只是在自己内心感受他们渴望成为整体、成为海洋本身的愿望——这本身就无比圆满。
