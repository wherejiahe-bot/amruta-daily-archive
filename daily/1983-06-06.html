Jun 6, 1983

Ego satisfied with own shadow

自我满足于自身的阴影
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
English
It's not the problem that you have few people, it's not the problem that Sahaja Yoga cannot give you Realization, it's not the problem that you will not know the Truth, but the whole circumstance is such, that whatever is false, whatever is spurious, whatever is silly, stupid, appeals to the masses.
中文
问题不在于人少，不在于霎哈嘉瑜伽无法给你自觉，不在于你无法认识真理——而是整个情况如此：一切虚假的、伪劣的、愚蠢的、荒谬的东西，反而吸引大众。
English
Sometimes I feel I have developed a method by which I can give really en masse Realization all over the world. Definitely I have. But the problem as it remains, the masses are not interested. It's only very few who really can keenly take interest into their evolutionary process and would like to come out of the ignorance in which they have been living.
中文
有时候我觉得我已找到一种方法，可以在全世界范围大规模地给予自觉。确实可以。但问题依然存在——大众并不感兴趣。只有极少数人真正渴望关注自己的进化过程，愿意走出长久以来蒙蔽他们的无知。
English
In all the ego-oriented countries it's very difficult to establish, that we are still ignorant, we have to know a lot. It's very difficult. Because they think we know everything, because we have been to the moon like the Russians say, 'We never saw God, we went to the moon, we never saw God.' It would be like saying that I have come to the third floor, I haven't seen God.
中文
在所有以自我为中心的国家里，很难让人们承认：我们仍然无知，我们还有许多要学。非常难。因为他们认为自己无所不知——就像俄国人说'我们去了月球，从没见过上帝'一样。这就像说'我走到了三楼，我没见到上帝'。
English
Where does God reside? Where it manifest within us? In what way it manifest, we don't want to see. What we want to see is that through our effort, if we have not been able to see God, then He doesn't exist. This is the way our attitude is.
中文
上帝住在哪里？祂在我们内在如何显现？以何种方式显现——我们不愿去看。我们想看的是：通过我们自己的努力，如果没能见到上帝，那么祂就不存在。这就是我们的态度。
English
But better say, 'No,' to all that we did not know through our effort, because so far we have not known about Him. So He doesn't exist. So you turn round in a cave when you see the shadows of yourself, you believe in them only and say, 'It never existed anything else, the light does not exist.' That kind of an attitude exists or is persistently I am facing.
中文
不如对我们未能通过努力认知的一切说'不'——因为我们至今不了解祂，所以祂不存在。于是你在洞穴里转过身，只看到自己的影子，你只相信这些影子，说：'从来不存在别的东西，光不存在。'这种态度一直存在，也是我持续面对的。
