Expanding the awareness

扩大觉知
Source: www.sahaja.live
◇
Now, as Sahaja Yogis, you are a powerful entities.
作为霎哈嘉瑜伽士，你是强大的存在体。
You are very powerful people.
你是非常有力量的人。
We have got problems in the whole world.
我们在整个世界都有问题。
You know that very well.
你非常了解这一点。
It’s not necessary you have to be great intellectuals, or you have to be people who are some sort of politicians, or something.
你不必成为伟大的学者或成为某种政治家，或什么人物。
But you must be on the lookout for the problems which are troubling this world.
但你必须警惕这些问题，这些在烦扰这个世界的问题。
You have to be.
你一定要。
You cannot live in your own world, that you are a saint, “Now I am in nirananda!” that’s not possible.
你不能只生活在你作为圣人的自己的世界里，“现在我处于纯粹喜乐中！”这不可能。
You can’t just be in niranand. [Laughter.]
你不能只在纯粹喜乐中。
You have to know that you have to live in this world, and you have to know all the problems of this world.
你要知道你必须活在这个世界里，而且你要知道这个世界存在的所有问题。
And you have to think about them, not only your problems that, “Mother how will I be able to do this and how will I be able to do that?”
你也要考虑这些问题，而不只是自己的问题“母亲，我怎样才能做这个？我怎样才能做那个？”
But you have to worry about the whole world.
你要挂虑整个世界。
You have to think, what’s happening in the world, what are the problems of the world.
你要思索，这世界正在发生着什么，这世界的问题是什么。
You are responsible for that.
你对此负有责任。
Not only that, but you have to pray.
不仅如此 你还要祈祷。
In your prayers you have to say, “Mother solve this problem.”
祈祷中你一定要说，“母亲，请解决这个问题。”
Collectively, individually, you have to take your attention from yourself, from your smaller life, to a much wider thing – then you are a saint.
以集体，以个人你要将你的注意力从自身，从你小的生活转移到更广大的事物——你是一个圣人。
And you have to think that it is your duty to ask for Divine help, that all these problems should be solved.
你必须想到，请求上天的帮助是你的义务，所有这些问题都应被解决。
This is your job for which you are selected.
这就是你被拣选要做的工作。
