太初导师崇拜–建立导师原则

The Guru Principle is to be maintained

1982-07-04

The wisdom or the essence of Guru Tattwa is a balance.

导师原则(Guru tattwa)的智慧或精粹是平衡。

Like when you maintain a plant, if you do not give it water, it will die. If you give it too much water, it will die.

就如你要保养植物，你不给它水，它会死；你给它太多水，它也会死。

So the wisdom lies in understanding how much water should be given to the plant, so that it comes up at its best.

所以智慧就是你懂得给恰当的水给植物，令它生长得最好。

Now this wisdom has to be achieved through your vibratory awareness.

智慧要以你的生命能量的感知力去达成。

You are placed under the most fortunate circumstances, that you are realised souls before your Guru Tattwa is fully established.

你是被安置在最幸运的境况，在你的导师原则完全建立巩固前，你已是个有自觉的灵。

You have all the powers that any guru can aspire, without having the establishment of Guru Tattwa.

在导师原则还未建立巩固前，你已拥有所有导师渴求的力量。

Specially you are under the complete blanket of the clouds of your Mother's love.

特别是你被母亲爱的祥云完全包裹覆盖。

But in no way Mother's love is going to allow you to destroy your Guru Principle.

母亲的爱绝不容许你毁灭摧毁你的导师原则。

As I told you that Guru Principle is a very sensitive thing, and is destroyed if you yourself do not try to maintain it.

就如我告诉你，导师原则是很易受伤害的，若你不保养它，它便会被毁灭。

First of all, the destruction starts from the very source of our being.

首先，毁灭源自你的存在体。

I mean to say that our being is created by five elements, and the five elements have a capacity all the time to get destroyed.

我是说我们的存在体是由五大原素所构成，这五大原素很容易被毁灭。

Any one of these elements if they are not looked after, they get destroyed.

若你不好好照顾任何一种原素，它们便会被毁灭。

That is the built-in capacity of the elements or of the matter, as you can say.

这是这些原素或物质内在的品质力量，我们也可以说。

So to maintain them is important.

保养它们是很重要的。

Some people have an idea that once you become a guru you need not maintain everything, it is maintained by itself.

有些人以为一旦你变成导师，便不用保养维持一切，它自会自动保养自己。

It is not.

不是这样。

Agreed that the spirit definitely enhances the beauty and the health of these elements; but at the human level there is always a greater force that is trying to destroy it.

我同意灵是绝对能增添这些原素的美丽，令它们健康。可是在人类的层次，常常都有一个更大的力量想去摧毁它。

So till we have completely become the spirit, there is always a chance of this destruction working it out.

所以除非我们完全变成灵，否则这个毁灭力量仍有机会起作用。

One may say, why this balance needed?

有人或许会说，为什么需要平衡？

And this is an important question which we should be able to answer all the people who ask us.

这是个很重要的问题，我们要懂回答。

Without the balance you cannot ascend.

没有平衡，你便不能升进。

If you cannot ascend, what's the use of our becoming human beings?

若你不能升进，成为人类又有什么用呢？
